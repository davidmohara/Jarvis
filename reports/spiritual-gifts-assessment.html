Your Profile: Strategic Leader + Strategist + Learner

You excel at seeing what needs to happen and making it happen. Your gifts center on vision, execution, wisdom, and an unshakeable trust in God. You organize complexity into systems, make wise decisions grounded in Scripture and facts, and create welcoming environments.

Your strengths: Macro-level leadership and strategy, decision-making under uncertainty, building organizational systems, learning and integration.

Your growth areas: Relational intimacy, direct service, and evangelistic drive aren't natural strengths—focus on what you do best.
