# Jorge Pliego: public research (verbatim agent report, 2026-11-03)

IDENTITY CONFIRMED. Jorge Pliego Seguin. LinkedIn lists Dallas, MIT, Improving. Improving bio says he joined in 2022. The SEC record (CIK 0001878923) and the ITAM/European University/MIT education track match the Improving bio, so the AgileThought CFO and Improving's CFO are the same person.

## Career timeline
- Improving, CFO, 2022 to present. Owns financial strategy plus organic and inorganic growth. (improving.com/profile/jorge-pliego/)
- Improving is PE-backed by Trinity Hunt Partners. Pliego, on the record: the company grew "to roughly $100 million before private equity joined us." Founders Chris Hyatt (CEO) and Rick Diana (M&A) still run it day to day. (cfothoughtleader.com, ep. 1153) Trinity Hunt closed a $618M continuation fund covering Improving and Argano in Oct 2022 (PR Newswire, 2022-10-21).
- AgileThought (Irving, TX), CFO of Legacy AT July 2016 to Aug 2021, then CFO of the public entity from Aug 2021. Salary $330,000. (SEC S-1/A, 2021-12-09)
- The public listing was a SPAC, not an IPO. AgileThought listed by merging with LIV Capital Acquisition Corp (LIVK), a Mexican SPAC. The former name "LIV Capital Acquisition Corp." runs to 2021-08-19 on EDGAR, which dates the closing. (data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001790625.json)
- Pre-2016: Diageo SVP Finance, West Latin America (May 2013 to Oct 2015); Diageo SVP Audit & Risk, Americas (Sep 2010 to Apr 2013); Avery Dennison Director of Finance, South America; Kellogg Treasury Manager (Jul 2000 to May 2001); earlier Procter & Gamble and Sara Lee Mexico, where he built the apparel division. Summarized as "over 30 years in large FMCG's." (S-1/A; improving.com; Hispanic Executive, 2025-07-28)
- Education: ITAM (Mexico City) finance degree, MBA European University Barcelona, executive education MIT. Age 57 as of the Dec 2021 S-1/A, so roughly 62 now.
- Board: Unidos Somos Iguales, a Mexico-based disability inclusion nonprofit. (improving.com)

## Public statements (best episode material)
- AI, from CFO Thought Leader ep. 1153: AI is "a very hungry monster. You feed it data, it gives you output, and then it immediately wants more." He argues clients should start with data quality and platform modernization, not AI tools. Improving built an internal tool called "Explorer" that scans client platforms for inconsistent data; on one large financial institution it "surfaced about 60 projects they need to address before an AI solution will generate real benefits." Improving does not sell Explorer. (cfothoughtleader.com/cfopodcasts/1153-from-big-company-discipline-to-private-equity-speed-jorge-pliego-cfo-improving/)
- M&A discipline, Hispanic Executive, 2025-07-28: "We, for the most part, purchase companies a fraction of Improving's size. Even if we make a very wrong decision, 10 percent of our revenue is the worst of the downside. It limits exposure." And: "Even if their numbers are great, if we feel the team we're looking to acquire is not a great fit for Improving, we don't move ahead." Also "Bringing structure and order to chaos is something I like to do." He runs a team of about 40.
- Priorities, CFO Thought Leader "Freedom to Succeed" episode: faster closes, utilization focus, and "refinancing to fund aggressive M&A this year." (cfothoughtleader.com/cfopodcasts/freedom-to-succeed-jorge-pliego-cfo-improving/)
