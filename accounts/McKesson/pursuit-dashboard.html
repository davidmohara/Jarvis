9-Box Methodology

Potential (4.1, High): Geography 4 × 0.10 = 0.4 (both flags Y: Dallas TX enterprise + Improving Dallas office). Revenue 5 × 0.70 = 3.5 (capped — ~$137M estimated IT services spend via industry-benchmark fallback against MMS's disclosed $11.4B FY2025 segment revenue, far above the table's $20M ceiling). Gross Margin 1 × 0.20 = 0.2 (35% default, interpolated toward 34%→1).

Realized (-3.3, Low): Geography 4 × 0.10 = 0.4 (same structural flags). Revenue -5 × 0.70 = -3.5 ($634,200 won since 1/15/2022, annualized to ~$141K/yr — below the $200K floor, capped). Gross Margin -1 × 0.20 = -0.2 (30% default).

Workflow issue flagged, not smoothed over: the Revenue factor's fallback benchmark (industry % × company revenue) breaks down for large accounts — it's functionally a constant +5 ceiling regardless of whether the target is a $1B or $400B company, same issue previously flagged on the Schwab pursuit map run. This 9-box was deliberately scored on McKesson Medical Surgical specifically (not the $403B parent), matching this plan's expansion framing.
