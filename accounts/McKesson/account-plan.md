### Delta 1. Cybersecurity incident (confirmed, McKesson 8-K and company disclosure page)

On **August 25, 2026** McKesson discovered a cybersecurity incident affecting its information systems. The company filed an 8-K under Item 7.01 on August 28, 2026 stating that the incident was in its early stages of investigation and that it had **not** determined the incident to be material. Disclosure timeline per the company's own incident page:

- **August 28, 2026:** initial disclosure, described as unauthorized access and exfiltration of data involving third-party applications.
- **August 29, 2026:** company confirmed it continued serving customers, accepting orders, and shipping product; distribution centers remained operational.
- **September 21, 2026:** investigation, supported by outside cybersecurity experts, confirmed the unauthorized access and exfiltration were **limited to certain third-party applications** affecting **a subset of customers within the Oncology & Multispecialty business**, and that the data involved was **primarily business contact and order data connected to the Medical-Surgical business**.

**Why this matters to this plan specifically:** the confirmed data set is Medical-Surgical order and contact data, which is David's own sub-account. This is not adjacent news; it lands on the exact business unit this pursuit map is built around.

**Attack-vector and scale reporting (needs-verification, trade press only):** press reporting attributes the intrusion to the ShinyHunters extortion group and to an identity/vishing vector involving Okta, with the attacker claiming 284 million patient records. The Register reported approximately 6.4 million records exposed as of September 10, 2026. **McKesson has not publicly confirmed a record count.** Treat the attacker's 284 million figure as a claim, not a fact, and do not repeat it to a client.

**Sourcing:** [Form 8-K, August 28, 2026 (event date August 25, 2026)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000927653/000092765326000247/mck-20260825.htm); [McKesson cybersecurity incident page](https://www.mckesson.com/cybersecurity); BleepingComputer (Aug 28, 2026), CyberScoop (Aug 31, 2026), Healthcare Dive (Sep 2, 2026), The Register (Sep 10, 2026), Rescana Okta/cloud breach analysis (Aug 30, 2026). ### Delta 4. The Medical-Surgical separation is now contractually and financially real, not just announced

This is the most consequential update for David's own sub-account, and it changes the strength of Option A materially. Four confirmed facts:

4. **The Wellverse name is now confirmed in a primary source.** The August 5, 2026 earnings release states: "On August 5, 2026, McKesson unveiled Wellverse as the name of the future standalone Medical-Surgical Solutions business." The August 2026 plan carried this from trade press; it is now company-confirmed.
