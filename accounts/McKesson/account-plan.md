## McKesson's Stated Strategic Priorities

Sourced from investor day materials, SEC filings, and press releases. Four named, cited themes:

### 1. Medical-Surgical Separation — the Wellverse Carve-Out
McKesson is separating its Medical-Surgical Solutions segment (FY2025 revenue: $11.4B) into an independent company to "unlock value for McKesson shareholders." Apollo Funds committed $1.25B for a ~13% stake (April 20, 2026, ~$13B MMS valuation). The unit will operate as **Wellverse** starting January 2027, targeting an IPO in H2 CY2027 followed by a spin-off/split-off of McKesson's remaining interest. This is the single most consequential, most time-pressured priority for David's own account.
Sources: [McKesson FY2025 Q4/FY Results — Intent to Separate Medical-Surgical Solutions](https://www.mckesson.com/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2025/mckesson-reports-fiscal-2025-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-results/); [McKesson Signs Agreement with Apollo Funds for Strategic Minority Interest in Medical-Surgical Solutions](https://www.mckesson.com/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2026/mckesson-signs-agreement-with-apollo-funds-for-strategic-minority-interest-in-medical-surgical-solutions/); [Richmond BizSense — McKesson's IPO-bound spin-off to rebrand as Wellverse](https://richmondbizsense.com/2026/08/06/mckesson-medical-surgical-to-keep-local-hq-after-spin-off-from-parent-company-rebranding-as-wellverse/); [Virginia Business — Henrico's McKesson Medical-Surgical plans 2027 IPO](https://virginiabusiness.com/mckesson-medical-surgical-rebrands-spinoff/)

### 2. Oncology, Multispecialty & Biopharma Growth
At the September 23, 2025 Investor Day, McKesson raised its long-term Adjusted EPS growth target to 13-16% (from 12-14%), citing "differentiated assets and capabilities across oncology, multispecialty, and biopharma services." The US Oncology Network added 570+ providers in FY2026 — its largest net increase since 2010. This is the growth engine McKesson is redirecting capital toward as it sheds Medical-Surgical.
Sources: [McKesson Investor Day, September 23, 2025](https://www.mckesson.com/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2025/mckesson-corporation-highlights-strategic-growth-strategies-and-unveils-updated-long-term-financial-targets-at-investor-day/); [McKesson Reports Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter and Full Year Results](https://www.mckesson.com/about-us/newsroom/press-releases/2026/mckesson-reports-fiscal-2026-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-results/)

### 3. AI Embedded in Core Operations
McKesson is deploying AI as operational infrastructure rather than a pilot layer: an AI-driven planning system orchestrating demand, supply, inventory, and operations contributed to $6.2B in FY2026 operating cash flow; ambient-scribe technology is in use by 1,900+ US Oncology Network providers to reduce documentation burden.
Sources: [Distribution Strategy Group — McKesson Posts Record Revenues, Drives AI Deep into Distribution Operations](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/05/mckesson-posts-record-revenues-drives-ai-deep-into-distribution-operations/)

### 4. Technology Services & Cloud/SAP Modernization
McKesson runs one of the largest SAP footprints in healthcare and is mid-way through a multi-year SAP S/4HANA migration, having moved its SAP-on-Azure environment for a 30-40% performance gain, while designating Google Cloud Platform as its preferred cloud provider for its broader infrastructure/analytics modernization (with Snowflake replicated cross-cloud to GCP for resilience). In October 2023, McKesson restructured IT leadership specifically to "increase alignment" behind its technology-services growth, folding the CIO/CTO role (now Francisco Fraga) under the CFO's oversight.
Sources: [Microsoft Customer Stories — McKesson takes SAP ecosystem to Azure](https://www.microsoft.com/en/customers/story/24058-mckesson-azure); [Google Cloud Blog — McKesson chooses Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/customers/mckesson-chooses-google-cloud-to-help-it-chart-a-course-to-the-future); [Snowflake Blog — McKesson's Cross-Cloud Data Journey](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/mckessons-cross-cloud-data-journey/); [CIO Dive — McKesson revamps IT leadership amid tech services push](https://www.ciodive.com/news/mckesson-brett-vitalone-francisco-fraga/695529/)
