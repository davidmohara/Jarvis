Silicon Labs Pre-Close Integration Readiness

TI's $7.5B all-cash acquisition of Silicon Labs (announced Feb 4, 2026) is its largest deal ever. Close is now guided to H1 2027, with China reportedly heading to a Phase 2 review, so TI is sitting in a longer-than-planned pre-close window. That window is when integration-office work, systems inventories, and day-one readiness get funded and done. Grounded offering: Data Enablement Engagement (4-8 weeks, $125,000-$250,000, fixed scope) for the systems-and-data inventory workstream, plus Application Modernization (application assessment and modernization roadmap). Source: SharePoint Sales Offerings tree, pulled live 2026-10-23. The sellable shape is a bounded, fixed-price readiness engagement, not an open-ended delivery pod.
“Your close is now guided to the first half of 2027, which means the integration work that has to be finished before day one is running on a longer runway than the announcement assumed. We would propose one bounded piece of it: a four-to-eight-week, fixed-price engagement that builds the systems and data inventory and the migration-readiness roadmap for a single pre-close workstream. Where is that pre-close work most under-resourced right now?”
Why lead here: It sells into integration budget that exists today rather than IT budget that opens at close, it maps to real, priced catalog offerings (Data Enablement Engagement; Application Modernization) rather than an invented capability, and it survives a regulatory delay instead of being killed by one.

Grounded offering: Data Enablement Engagement (4-8 weeks, $125K-$250K), whose stated shape is exactly a scoped discovery-plus-build for data pipelines and reporting.

“You are adding capacity at Sherman, at Lehi, at the Malaysia back-end site, and now at Richardson, all while back-end lead times stretch. Where is the manufacturing-systems and data side of that ramp feeling the most strain today?”
