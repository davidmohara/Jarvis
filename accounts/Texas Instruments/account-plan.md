## Texas Instruments' Stated Strategic Priorities

Sourced from the FY2025 10-K (filed February 6, 2026), the February 4, 2026 Silicon Labs acquisition announcement, Q1/Q2 2026 earnings call transcripts, and TI's own capital-management investor materials. Four themes.

### 1. Edge AI and Embedded Connectivity, Anchored by the Silicon Labs Acquisition
TI's $7.5B all-cash acquisition of Silicon Labs (announced Feb 4, 2026, enterprise value ~$7.5B) is explicitly positioned to "create a global leader in embedded wireless connectivity solutions," combining Silicon Labs' mixed-signal/connectivity IP with TI's analog and embedded-processing portfolio as the IoT ecosystem "pivots toward edge AI computing." Silicon Labs' Series 3 platform is cited by name as delivering a 10x AI-performance uplift (100 AI GOPS vs. ~10 GOPS/300 in the prior generation). TI also frames the deal as leveraging its own internally-owned, low-cost 300mm manufacturing capacity (Sherman, Texas) to scale Silicon Labs' ~1,200-product wireless connectivity portfolio.
Sources: [Texas Instruments to acquire Silicon Labs — TI.com press release, Feb 4, 2026](https://www.ti.com/about-ti/newsroom/news-releases/2026/2026-02-04-texas-instruments-to-acquire-silicon-labs.html); [Texas Instruments to acquire Silicon Labs — Investor Relations](https://investor.ti.com/news-releases/news-release-details/texas-instruments-acquire-silicon-labs); [What TI's Acquisition of Silicon Labs Stands For? — EE Times](https://www.eetimes.com/what-ti-acquisition-of-silicon-labs-stands-for/); [Texas Instruments Buys Silicon Labs To Fuel Edge AI Scale — Futurum Group](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/texas-instruments-buys-silicon-labs-to-fuel-edge-ai-scale/)

### 2. Completing and Ramping the $60B+ U.S. Manufacturing Buildout (Sherman, TX and Lehi, UT)
TI has invested more than $60B across seven 300mm wafer fabs in Texas (Sherman) and Utah (Lehi) — the largest investment in foundational semiconductor manufacturing in U.S. history, supporting as many as 3,000 direct jobs at the Sherman site alone. TI's FY2025 10-K and capital-management materials describe the current phase as ramping production and qualification at these newest fabs, with capital expenditure now declining as the six-year build-out cycle ends (2026 CapEx guided to $2–5B, down from the peak investment years), explicitly to support higher free cash flow.
Sources: [TI reports Q4 2025 and 2025 financial results and shareholder returns](https://investor.ti.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ti-reports-q4-2025-and-2025-financial-results-and-shareholder); [Texas Instruments Capital Management Review — Investor Relations](https://investor.ti.com/static-files/1890477c-9df1-4e66-b43a-80521e40e4e1); [Sherman, Texas: 300mm wafer fabs — TI.com](https://www.ti.com/about-ti/company/ti-at-a-glance/manufacturing/sherman.html); [Inside Texas Instruments' New 300mm Fab in Sherman, Texas — EE Times](https://www.eetimes.com/inside-texas-instruments-new-300mm-fab-in-sherman-texas/)

### 3. Capital Efficiency and Free Cash Flow Per Share as the Primary Financial Target
TI's stated long-term strategic focus is growth of free cash flow (FCF) per share as "the ultimate measure to generate value," targeting a 10% CAGR with a "significant step forward" expected in 2026 as CapEx declines from its investment-cycle peak. TI increased its dividend 4% to $1.42/share and repurchased $1.5B of shares in 2025, reaffirming a commitment to "return all free cash flow to owners over time." The August 1, 2026 CFO transition (Julie Knecht succeeding the retiring Rafael Lizardi) is explicitly framed around continuity on this exact priority — CEO Haviv Ilan credited Lizardi's "focus on disciplined capital allocation" and said Knecht shares that commitment.
Sources: [TI reports Q4 2025 and 2025 financial results and shareholder returns](https://investor.ti.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ti-reports-q4-2025-and-2025-financial-results-and-shareholder); [Texas Instruments announces CFO transition — TI.com, June 2, 2026](https://www.ti.com/about-ti/newsroom/news-releases/2026/2026-06-02-texas-instruments-announces-cfo-transition.html); [Texas Instruments Capital Management Presentation — Investor Relations](https://investor.ti.com/static-files/4e8d323e-83e6-4b70-88fd-b3e2b65969bd)

### 4. Internalizing Embedded Production and Operational Efficiency
TI's own stated strategy calls out rapid internalization of embedded-processing production, targeting over 90% in-house manufacturing by the end of the decade, paired with continued operational-efficiency gains as new 300mm capacity comes online. TI's Q2 2026 earnings call reported industrial demand up 30% YoY, automotive turning up mid-teens YoY, and data-center demand doubling YoY — end-market signals reinforcing the case for scaled, efficient internal production.
Sources: [Texas Instruments (TXN) 10K Form and Latest SEC Filings — MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NASDAQ/TXN/sec-filings/); [Texas Instruments Q2 2026 Earnings Call Transcript — Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4924432-texas-instruments-incorporated-txn-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript); [Earnings call transcript: Texas Instruments beats Q2 2026 estimates — Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-texas-instruments-beats-q2-2026-estimates-shares-fall-after-hours-93CH-4807116)

**Source set:** 10-K + 8-K (CFO transition, Silicon Labs deal) + earnings calls + press releases + investor capital-management materials (public company, standard source set — no reduction needed).
