## AT&T's Stated Strategic Priorities

**Updated 2026-10-23.** Sourced from AT&T's Q3 2026 earnings release and call transcript (2026-10-21), Q2 2026 materials, investor materials, and company press releases. These are company-level, primary-source facts, unaffected by the CRM territory correction above.

**AT&T's own framing.** The Q3 2026 earnings deck states three business priorities verbatim: "Grow converged customer relationships," "Operate with greater efficiency," and "Invest for durable growth and attractive returns." [3Q26_ATT_Earnings_Slides.pdf, investors.att.com, 2026-10-21] The five themes below are the operating expression of those three.

**1. AI-Grade Connectivity. NEW as the lead theme at this refresh.**

This is the most consequential change since August. AT&T has moved from describing an "AI-ready network" ambition to selling a named enterprise product architecture. On the Q3 2026 call, CEO John Stankey framed fiber as the AI asset: "Over 90% of mapped US data centers are within 10 miles of our fiber and over 80% are within 1 mile. This is one of the reasons why the nation's leading hyperscalers are working with AT&T to support their rapidly growing high-capacity connectivity needs. This includes AWS, who is using AT&T to connect their locations with high-capacity fiber. We're also working with Microsoft to support its high-capacity needs as AI growth drives demand." He described layering "high-margin services and network intelligence on top of that fiber to connect growing AI workloads across the entire ecosystem, including business endpoints, metro aggregation, and managed multi-vendor cloud ingress and egress." [Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 2026-10-21]

The product side is now public. A three-part AT&T Business blog series published through October 2026 defines the **"AI-grade network"** as three layers plus an operating layer: the physical network, **Intelligent NaaS**, the **AI Fabric**, and **NetOps for AI**. The stated capability set is explicitly data and observability engineering: live topology across data center, cloud, and edge; token-level performance telemetry (time to first token, tokens per second, errors); anomaly detection and ranked root-cause correlation across network, cloud, security, and model signals; policy enforcement at the prompt; agent registries and per-action authorization; and cost attribution for AI spend. AT&T Business has opened an **MVP program** for customers to co-build this. [AT&T Business blog, "The AI-Grade Network: Where Bytes Meet Tokens," 2026-10-22, about.att.com/blogs/2026/ai-grade-network.html; business.att.com/portfolios/ai-networking.html]

**2. Fiber Expansion & Convergence Growth. UPDATED.**

AT&T reached more than **39 million** total consumer and business fiber locations in Q3 2026 and is on track to exceed **40 million by the end of 2026** and **more than 60 million by the end of 2030**, of which approximately 50 million will be AT&T owned and operated. The 60 million figure includes locations served through the new fiber JV and other open-access partners. [Q3 2026 earnings release, 2026-10-21; Fiber JV press release, 2026-10-06]

*Correction from the prior version:* the prior build cited a "50 million locations" target from the Q2 2026 call. The current, authoritative framing is 60 million total by 2030, with roughly 50 million owned and operated. Use the 60 million figure.

Convergence remains the commercial engine: **42.8%** of households with AT&T advanced home internet also take AT&T wireless. Q3 delivered a record 769,000 Advanced Connectivity internet net adds (442,000 fiber, 327,000 fixed wireless) and 379,000 postpaid phone net adds at 0.92% postpaid phone churn. Fiber ARPU declined 4% YoY on introductory pricing and converged discounts, and management guided that pressure to continue into Q4. [Q3 2026 earnings release and call transcript, 2026-10-21]

**3. Network Modernization and Spectrum Deployment. CONFIRMED, unchanged in substance.**

AT&T continues to push midband 5G coverage toward 300+ million POPs by end of 2026 and to deploy commercial-scale **Open RAN** with Ericsson, targeting 70% of wireless traffic on open-capable platforms. No new public update to the Open RAN percentage was issued this cycle, so the March 2026 figure stands as the last confirmed data point. [MarketBeat, AT&T CTO interview, 2026-03-28]

The EchoStar spectrum closed 2026-07-28 at $23 billion, taking net debt to adjusted EBITDA from 2.68x to **3.13x** in Q3. Management reaffirmed the target of returning to the 2.5x range during 2029, funded by EBITDA growth plus proceeds from the fiber JV and the Mexico sale. [Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 2026-10-21]

**4. Cost Transformation and Copper Retirement. UPDATED with hard dates.**

The **$4 billion in annual consolidated cost savings by end of 2028** target is reaffirmed, and copper retirement is named as the primary mechanism: "Copper retirement remains an integral part of our overall cost transformation, and we remain on pace to achieve our target of $4 billion in annual consolidated cost savings by the end of 2028." [Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 2026-10-21]

The schedule is now specific. AT&T has **more than 100 wire centers with no customers on legacy services**, and targets discontinuing copper-based services in **50% of wire centers outside California by end of 2027** and **substantially all of them by end of 2029**. Legacy segment revenue fell 24.5% YoY in Q3 and management expects legacy to be immaterial by end of 2029, with legacy EBITDA turning negative after 2027. [Q3 2026 earnings release and call transcript, 2026-10-21]

Q3 also carried a roughly **$800 million employee separations charge**, with cash impact primarily in 2027. [Q3 2026 earnings call transcript, 2026-10-21]

**5. Portfolio Reshaping: Buying Fiber and Spectrum, Selling Geography. UPDATED and broadened.**

The prior version framed this as inorganic capacity expansion only. It is now a two-directional portfolio story, which is a materially different selling context.

*Buying:* EchoStar spectrum closed 2026-07-28 ($23B). Lumen mass-markets fiber closed 2026-02-02 ($5.75B), now sitting in Forged Fiber 37. A multi-year Corning agreement valued at **more than $3 billion** for fiber and cable was signed 2026-09-29. [AT&T newsroom, "AT&T and Corning Team Up to Engineer the Connected World with Fiber," 2026-09-29]

*Restructuring:* Forged Fiber 37 and Gigapower are being merged into one wholesale open-access fiber JV with GIP and CPP Investments, AT&T at 50%, closing expected H1 2027. [Fiber JV press release, 2026-10-06]

*Selling:* AT&T Mexico is being sold to Grupo Televisa and Fintech Holdings for $2 billion, closing expected Q1 2027. Stankey: "We have articulated a strong belief that the AI future will run on converged networks with dense fiber assets. This move positions AT&T Mexico to participate in that evolution and is consistent with our long-term strategy to focus our capital allocation on opportunities for growth in the vibrant U.S. market." [AT&T newsroom, 2026-10-13]

**Uncited signal worth watching (not a stated priority):** AT&T has publicly called SpaceX's direct-to-phone strategy not "viable," and AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon formed a satellite joint venture on 2026-10-01 with Paul Roth as interim CEO to close coverage dead zones. Separately, AT&T and T-Mobile are reported to be tightening AI guardrails for autonomous network operations, with fully autonomous networks described as years away. These are secondary-source and trade-press signals, logged for positioning awareness rather than treated as stated strategic priorities. [AT&T newsroom, "AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon Launch Joint Venture That Helps End Dead Zones," 2026-10-01; Reuters, 2026-10-23]
