## AT&T's Stated Strategic Priorities

Sourced from AT&T's Q2 2026 earnings call and transcript, investor materials, and company press releases. These are company-level, primary-source facts — unaffected by the CRM territory correction above.

**1. Fiber Expansion & Convergence Growth.** AT&T is expanding its fiber footprint toward **50 million locations**, up from ~29 million at the end of 2025, adding over 1 million fiber locations in Q2 2026 alone. [Q2 2026 earnings call transcript, investors.att.com, 2026-07-22]

**2. 5G / Open RAN Network Modernization.** AT&T is pushing midband 5G coverage from ~270 million POPs (end of 2025) to 300+ million by end of 2026, deploying a commercial-scale **Open RAN** buildout with Ericsson, targeting **70% of wireless network traffic on open-capable platforms by late 2026**. [MarketBeat, AT&T CTO interview, 2026-03-28]

**3. AI-Ready Network Infrastructure.** CEO John Stankey has stated AT&T's ambition to operate "the most advanced and technologically open communications network in the U.S." by the end of the decade, with network investment aimed at low-latency, high-uplink-capacity connectivity to serve AI-driven traffic. [Seeking Alpha, "AT&T Outlines Strategic Plan to Drive Sustainable Growth," 2026]

**4. Cost Transformation.** Management is targeting **$4 billion in annual cost savings by the end of 2028**, with adjusted EPS targeted for a double-digit three-year CAGR through 2028. [Investing.com, "AT&T Q2 2026 slides: fiber strategy drives margin expansion"]

**5. Inorganic Capacity Expansion (Spectrum & Fiber M&A).** The EchoStar spectrum close (7/28/2026, $23B) and the Lumen mass-markets fiber close (2/2/2026, $5.75B) are direct evidence AT&T is buying capacity as fast as it is building it — both create near-term system integration, data migration, and application-rationalization work.
