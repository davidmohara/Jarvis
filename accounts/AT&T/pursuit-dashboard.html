  • Fiber Expansion & Convergence Growth: Expanding fiber footprint toward 50 million locations (up from ~29 million end of 2025), adding 1M+ fiber locations in Q2 2026 alone. Fiber + 5G convergence is the stated core growth engine.
  • 5G / Open RAN Network Modernization: Pushing midband 5G coverage from ~270M POPs (end of 2025) to 300M+ by end of 2026; deploying commercial-scale Open RAN with Ericsson, targeting 70% of wireless traffic on open-capable platforms by late 2026.
  • AI-Ready Network Infrastructure: CEO John Stankey has stated the ambition to operate "the most advanced and technologically open communications network in the U.S." by end of decade, framed explicitly as a monetization/service-differentiation opportunity.
  • Cost Transformation: Targeting $4 billion in annual cost savings by end of 2028, with adjusted EPS targeted for double-digit three-year CAGR through 2028.
  • Inorganic Capacity Expansion (Spectrum & Fiber M&A): The EchoStar spectrum close (7/28/2026, $23B) and Lumen mass-markets fiber close (2/2/2026, $5.75B) are direct, dated evidence AT&T is buying capacity as fast as it is building it.
