Customer ID and Loyalty Data Enablement

Grow from two embedded engineers into a scoped engagement supporting the 7NOW and digital roadmap. Strengthened this quarter by a dated, publicly scoped deliverable: customer ID integration and a store loyalty program, announced for 2H FY2026 with a roughly 100 million user target. It is also co-sellable: Databricks is 7-Eleven's confirmed data platform and an Improving partner. Renamed from "Digital Product Engineering Surge" by the offering gate: the sellable shape is a fixed-scope engagement, not an open-ended pod.
“You've put customer ID integration and a store loyalty program on the 2H roadmap with a 100-million-user target, on a Databricks platform we already know. Our two engineers have been in your org for five years without a hiccup. Let us take one fixed-scope slice of that build, four to eight weeks at a fixed price, and show you what an already-onboarded team does faster than a new hire or a new vendor.”
Why lead here: It runs entirely through the existing, trusted relationship (Wulfekammer, Ditzler) instead of cold executive outreach, and it is the only fully grounded play: it maps to the published Data Enablement Engagement, 4–8 weeks at $125K–$250K (offering gate, 10/23/2026). A standing pod afterward is the draft Embedded Delivery Capacity outline and needs CCO-office validation first. Sequence it after November 6: Seven & i publishes a new strategy and investment framework at IR Day that day, and pitching before it means pitching against a plan that is about to be replaced.
