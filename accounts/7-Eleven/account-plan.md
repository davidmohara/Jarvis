## Situation Summary

**Entity.** The target is **7-Eleven, Inc.**, the North American convenience-store operating company (7-Eleven + Speedway banners), headquartered at 3200 Hackberry Rd, Irving, TX 75063 — inside the Dallas-Fort Worth metro. 7-Eleven, Inc. is a wholly-owned subsidiary of **Seven & i Holdings Co., Ltd.** (Tokyo Stock Exchange: 3382), the Japan-listed parent. This is not ambiguous as a brand, but the corporate structure matters for sourcing: 7-Eleven, Inc. does not file its own 10-K — the closest primary-source equivalent is Seven & i Holdings' TSE disclosures and investor materials.

**Engagement shape: ACTIVE-BUT-UNDERLEVERAGED — this corrects the "cold start" framing the request came in with.** Improving's own internal account record (`projects/improving-accounts-overview.md`) shows a **stable two-person staff-augmentation presence, consistently extended since 2019, with no team growth**. **CRM-confirmed (live Dynamics pull, 8/7/2026): the account owner is Derek Nwamadi** — not David directly, confirming the internal record. **Zero open opportunities currently.** Historical relationship: 39 of 133 total opportunities won since account creation (4/11/2017), $4,089,017 in cumulative closed-won revenue since 1/15/2022 (per the account's Dynamics AI-generated summary). **True TTM (trailing twelve months, 8/7/2025–8/7/2026) realized revenue is $184,800** — two won opportunities: "Suren Bafna Extension" ($129,600, closed 10/10/2025) and "Josue Aguilar" ($55,200, closed 10/7/2025). A third opportunity, "Josue Extension with 7-Eleven" ($115,920, closed 7/7/2025), falls one month outside the strict TTM window and is excluded from the confirmed figure but noted as directionally relevant. This is the Schwab pattern (existing beachhead, underrepresented in capability areas), not the Constellation pattern (cold/lost re-entry) — but the CRM-confirmed TTM figure is materially lower than the provisional estimate this plan originally carried, reinforcing just how underleveraged the account actually is relative to its five-year-plus relationship and $4M+ lifetime revenue.

**Timing trigger.** Three stacked, dated events make now credible:
1. **Mauricio Leyva named CEO of 7-Eleven, Inc., effective August 1, 2026** — six days before this plan was written — succeeding Joe DePinto, who retired at the end of 2025 after more than two decades leading the company. New leadership almost always reopens vendor and budget conversations that had calcified under a long-tenured predecessor.
2. **Seven & i's "Turning Point 2026" transformation plan**, including a stated global technology strategy and a new Center of Excellence for business transformation, presented by Seven & i President & CEO Stephen Hayes Dacus (the company's first foreign CEO) — most recently detailed in the July 9, 2026 investor presentation.
3. **The planned IPO/spinoff of 7-Eleven's North American business in H2 2026** — following Alimentation Couche-Tard's withdrawal of its ~$47B acquisition bid for Seven & i in July 2025. Status is genuinely uncertain (C-Store Dive reports analysts questioning whether the IPO is still necessary now that the defensive rationale is gone) — flagged as an open item, not treated as settled.

The account's real story: five years of quiet, stable staff-aug work sitting underneath a company about to reset its C-suite, its technology strategy, and possibly its corporate structure. The opening is to move from "the vendor nobody thinks about" to "the delivery partner who shows up as the new leadership resets priorities."

## 7-Eleven / Seven & i's Stated Strategic Priorities

Sourced from Seven & i Holdings' July 9, 2026 investor presentation, its September 2025 "Plan for Transformation of 7-Eleven," and 2026 press coverage of CEO Dacus's stated priorities. 7-Eleven, Inc. does not file a US 10-K; Seven & i's Tokyo-listed disclosures are the closest primary-source equivalent — noted as a reduced-but-real source set.

**1. "Turning Point 2026" — Four Growth Priorities.** CEO Dacus has named four explicit 2026 priorities: delivering value that exceeds customer expectations, strengthening digital convenience and the last-mile experience, advancing food leadership, and controlling costs. (Source: Seven & i July 9, 2026 investor presentation; CSP Daily News, "Seven & i CEO Sets 2026 Priorities for Convenience Retail.")

**2. Full-Stack Digital & AI Transformation.** 7-Eleven consolidated its digital and IT leadership under a single EVP, Chief Digital and Information Officer (Raghu Mahadevan), and has publicly described a multi-year "full stack transformation" spanning consumer-facing technology, back-end systems, infrastructure, and the organizational stack itself. (Source: Metis Strategy interview series on 7-Eleven's CDO/CIO function.)

**3. AI-Enabled Personalization on the Microsoft/Azure Stack.** 7-Eleven has an existing, named partnership with Microsoft embedding Azure AI services into the 7NOW delivery app for personalized recommendations, with both companies stating an intent to keep expanding into AI, data analytics, and other intelligent-technology use cases. (Source: Microsoft/7-Eleven joint press release, PRNewswire — note: exact original announcement date not confirmed this session; treat as an established, ongoing relationship rather than a brand-new signal.)

**4. Cost Control Amid a Tough Retail Environment.** Cost discipline is explicitly one of the four named 2026 priorities, and is corroborated by 7-Eleven's own announced plan to close 645 underperforming North American stores in fiscal 2026 against only ~205 planned new openings. This matches Improving's own internal read on the account ("tough environment," no open pipeline) — the priority and the account's current state agree. (Sources: Seven & i July 2026 investor presentation; Yahoo Finance/Reuters coverage of the fiscal 2026 store-closure plan.)

**Uncited signal, not a stated priority:** Secondary coverage speculates 7-Eleven's IPO could unlock fresh technology investment as a newly independent company builds its own vendor stack — plausible, but not something either company has stated publicly as a technology priority. Flagged for awareness only.
