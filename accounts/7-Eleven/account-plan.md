## Situation Summary

**Entity.** The target is **7-Eleven, Inc.**, the North American convenience-store operating company (7-Eleven + Speedway banners), headquartered at 3200 Hackberry Rd, Irving, TX 75063 — inside the Dallas-Fort Worth metro. 7-Eleven, Inc. is a wholly-owned subsidiary of **Seven & i Holdings Co., Ltd.** (Tokyo Stock Exchange: 3382), the Japan-listed parent. This is not ambiguous as a brand, but the corporate structure matters for sourcing: 7-Eleven, Inc. does not file its own 10-K — the closest primary-source equivalent is Seven & i Holdings' TSE disclosures and investor materials.

**Engagement shape: ACTIVE-BUT-UNDERLEVERAGED — this corrects the "cold start" framing the request came in with.** Improving's own internal account record (`projects/improving-accounts-overview.md`) shows a **stable two-person staff-augmentation presence, consistently extended since 2019, with no team growth**. **CRM-confirmed (live Dynamics pull, 8/7/2026): the account owner is Derek Nwamadi** — not David directly, confirming the internal record. **Zero open opportunities currently.** Historical relationship: 39 of 133 total opportunities won since account creation (4/11/2017), $4,089,017 in cumulative closed-won revenue since 1/15/2022 (per the account's Dynamics AI-generated summary). **True TTM (trailing twelve months, 8/7/2025–8/7/2026) realized revenue is $184,800** — two won opportunities: "Suren Bafna Extension" ($129,600, closed 10/10/2025) and "Josue Aguilar" ($55,200, closed 10/7/2025). A third opportunity, "Josue Extension with 7-Eleven" ($115,920, closed 7/7/2025), falls one month outside the strict TTM window and is excluded from the confirmed figure but noted as directionally relevant. This is the Schwab pattern (existing beachhead, underrepresented in capability areas), not the Constellation pattern (cold/lost re-entry) — but the CRM-confirmed TTM figure is materially lower than the provisional estimate this plan originally carried, reinforcing just how underleveraged the account actually is relative to its five-year-plus relationship and $4M+ lifetime revenue.

**CRM re-pull, 2026-10-23 (Dataverse Web API).** Zero open opportunities confirmed, and a harder finding: **no opportunity was created or closed on this account in calendar 2026 at all.** The two most recent wins remain the Suren Bafna Extension ($129,600) and Josue Aguilar ($55,200), and their actual close dates are both 2025-10-06 (this plan previously quoted the estimated close dates, 10/10 and 10/7). That makes the standing question to Derek, "are the two embedded engineers still on site," the single most important open item on the account.

Use the 200 figure when talking about economic closures and 645 only when quoting the headline forecast with its caveat.
- **The IPO was H2 2026 in the prior version. It is now FY2027 at the earliest.**
- **Fiscal calendar:** Seven & i's FY2026 ends February 28, 2027. H1 is March 1 to August 31, 2026. Prior versions of this plan were ambiguous about which fiscal year was being referenced.

## 7-Eleven / Seven & i's Stated Strategic Priorities

Refreshed October 23, 2026 from Seven & i Holdings' **Q2 FY2026 presentation and Q&A session (October 8, 2026)**, the **Q1 FY2026 presentation (July 9, 2026)**, and Seven & i press releases. 7-Eleven, Inc. does not file a US 10-K; Seven & i's Tokyo-listed disclosures are the closest primary-source equivalent, noted as a reduced-but-real source set. The company's own label for the group program is the **"Transformation Plan"** and for the US operating company the **"North Star Plan."**

**1. Transformation Plan / North Star Plan, with a strategy reset due November 6, 2026.**
The operating company's plan is built on four visible levers: accelerate hot food and reinvent the open-air case, optimize the store network (close 200, open 205, convert 390 to franchise, add 350 wholesale fuel locations in FY2026), expand 7NOW, and convert company-operated stores to franchise to lift merchandise margin. Management states the framework itself is being updated and will be announced "around our IR Day in November." Dacus, October 8: "disciplined execution of the Transformation Plan remains our foremost priority." (Sources: Q2 FY2026 presentation, Oct 8, 2026, https://www.7andi.com/en/ir/file/library/ks/pdf/2026_1008kse_01.pdf; C-Store Dive, Oct 12, 2026.)

**2. Traffic and the customer, not cost, is now the named problem.**
The stated gap is customer traffic. Dacus, October 8: "We haven't turned the tide yet in terms of surpassing the previous year's level... it's really the North Star plan which is going to drive traffic positive." US same-store merchandise sales were +0.6% for H1 with Q2 roughly flat, and fresh food was still -0.3% in Q2. Third-party data shows 7-Eleven losing share of the lunch daypart to quick-service competitors. (Sources: Q2 Q&A, Oct 8, 2026; C-Store Dive, Oct 12 and Oct 15, 2026.)

**3. Digital: customer ID, a store loyalty program, and AI and robotics in store operations.**
This replaces the prior "full-stack transformation" framing with what the company actually said this quarter. The Q2 deck names, for 2H FY2026 onward, "Customer ID integration / Introduction of a Store loyalty program / Large-scale joint promotions," targeting approximately 100 million users across a base described as 21,000 stores and 20 million customer visits per day. Mid-to-long-term it names "transforming store operations through AI and robotics" and "building next-generation infrastructure." Group CFO Tetsuya Takagi said the July 2026 partnerships with SoftBank, PayPay, LY Corporation and Sumitomo Mitsui Card are aimed at "personalized customer experiences and a store loyalty program." (Sources: Q2 FY2026 presentation, Oct 8, 2026; Q&A session, Oct 8, 2026.)

**4. North American supply chain is moving in-house. New this quarter, and the most concrete services-buying signal in the set.**
7-Eleven, Inc. is reviewing bringing parts of its North American supply chain in-house as inflation bites. (Sources: Reuters, Oct 9, 2026; Grocery Trade News, "7-Eleven Reviews In-House Supply Chain in North America," Oct 12, 2026; Finimize, Oct 9, 2026.)

**5. Capital allocation shifts toward growth, and there is money for it.**
The Q&A states "capital allocation should be weighted more heavily toward growth investments than ever before." H1 FY2026 delivered revenue of ¥5,460.2B (+16.3% like-for-like), operating income of ¥232.2B (+36.6%) and net income of ¥124.4B (+17.8%), against a Q2 quarter that was weaker on its own (net profit ¥63.84B, -12% year over year). FY2026 guidance is unchanged at net income of ¥278.0B. The company executed ¥100B of net buybacks in H1 and has ¥1.3T remaining against a ¥2T FY2030 target, and issued ¥110B of unsecured bonds on October 23, 2026. (Sources: Q2 FY2026 presentation and Q&A, Oct 8, 2026; Seven & i, "Notice Regarding the Issue of Unsecured Bonds," Oct 23, 2026, https://www.7andi.com/en/company/news/release/83873.html.)

**Microsoft/Azure partnership (downgraded this refresh).** 7-Eleven has a named, public Microsoft partnership embedding Azure AI services into the 7NOW app. It is real but dates from the 2019 era, and no current-period confirmation of the Azure relationship was found. The only 2026 Microsoft-related news is an Xbox/Forza Horizon 6 marketing crossover in October, which is not infrastructure and should not be read as an Azure signal. Treat Microsoft as an established relationship of uncertain current scope, and re-verify before using it in a pitch.

**Databricks is the confirmed modern data platform (new this refresh).** Convenience Store News (October 19, 2026) names Databricks as the enterprise data platform underpinning the business, with Databricks Mosaic AI behind customer briefs, content and targeted offers, and reports audience segmentation cut from over a week to hours. This is the clearest technology-spend signal in the account and the best co-sell fit Improving has.

**Reported but not company-confirmed (do not cite as stated strategy):** Nikkei Asia, via Retail Insight Network on October 12, 2026, reported a medium-term frame through FY2030 including roughly ¥3.2T of global growth investment, a network of about 100,000 stores, 1,300 new US stores and 7,000+ US remodels by 2030, and European expansion as a "fourth core retail market." None of this appears in any company document retrieved this cycle, and it may be superseded on November 6. Treat as directional only.

**Uncited signal, not a stated priority:** secondary coverage speculates the IPO could unlock fresh technology investment as a newly independent company builds its own vendor stack. With the listing now pushed to FY2027 at the earliest, this is weaker than it was in August and remains flagged for awareness only.

### Full narrative profiles — the 5 most strategically relevant contacts

**1. Mauricio Leyva — CEO, 7-Eleven, Inc.**
*Background:* Two-plus decades in consumer products; most recently Group President at Keurig Dr Pepper (2020–2024). Appointment announced July 23, 2026 and in seat from roughly August 1, 2026, succeeding a 20-plus-year incumbent. As of this refresh he is about twelve weeks into the job, which changes the conversation from "welcome aboard" to "what have you decided."
*Role inference (labeled — not confirmed by primary source):* A CPG growth-and-brand-building background suggests a mandate focused on customer experience, food/beverage leadership, and modernizing a legacy retail operation. Publicly he has framed the job around redefining convenience, and has tied US IPO timing to progress on the turnaround, which raises the stakes on visible operational and technology wins inside his first year.
*Pitch angle:* "You've said the IPO timing depends on how the turnaround tracks. The constraint inside a turnaround is usually not strategy, it's delivery capacity. Where are you short of hands right now?"
*What to avoid:* Don't lead with the existing staff-aug arrangement as a credential, he doesn't know it exists yet. Don't reach him cold; this is an aspirational, later-sequence conversation, not a first move. Do not open with "congratulations on the new role," that window closed in August.

**2. Raghu Mahadevan — EVP, Chief Digital and Information Officer**
*Background:* Holds the consolidated Chief Digital + Information Officer role at 7-Eleven — publicly described as leading a multi-year "full stack transformation."
*Role inference:* Almost certainly the actual budget authority for any technology capability expansion beyond staff-aug headcount — likely constrained by the same "tough environment" cost pressure the internal account record flags.
*Pitch angle:* "You've been running a full-stack transformation — consumer tech, back-end, infrastructure, org — for a while now, and Improving's had two engineers embedded in your organization since 2019 without you probably knowing it. What's the piece of that transformation that's hardest to staff right now?"
*What to avoid:* Don't pitch as new business — acknowledge the existing (if small) footprint. Don't approach cold before Eric Wulfekammer or Matthew Ditzler have opened the door — he's a step-two conversation, not step one.

**3. Eric Wulfekammer — Sr. Director, IT Vendor Management**
*Background:* Existing Improving relationship contact; owns formal IT vendor sourcing/management. Likely the person whose sign-off has kept the 2019-era staff-aug arrangement alive and renewed for five years without incident.
*Role inference:* The most direct, lowest-friction path to any near-term expansion — he already trusts Improving enough to keep renewing.
*Pitch angle:* "It's been five years of quiet, reliable delivery on the same two-person team. With a new CEO and a transformation push coming, is now the moment to talk about growing that footprint rather than just renewing it?"
*What to avoid:* Don't treat this as a cold vendor-management conversation — acknowledge the tenure and ask what's changed on his side, not just pitch expansion.

**4. Matthew Ditzler — Director of Software Engineering**
*Background:* Existing Improving relationship contact; most likely the working-level manager the two embedded Improving engineers report into functionally.
*Role inference:* The person who actually feels day-to-day capacity constraints on the 7NOW/digital product roadmap — closest working-level entry to Priority 2 (digital convenience/last-mile).
*Pitch angle:* "Our two engineers have been heads-down in your org for years — what's the next thing on your roadmap that a senior, already-onboarded team could take on faster than hiring or a new vendor could?"
