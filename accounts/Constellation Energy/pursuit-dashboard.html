On Oct 6 2026 Constellation selected Google Cloud and Gemini Enterprise to embed agentic workflows in core operations. 9-Box Methodology

Potential (4.3, High): Geography 4 (wt. 0.10) + Revenue 5/capped (wt. 0.70) + Gross Margin 2 (wt. 0.20). Revenue estimate: FY2025 revenue $22.7B (pre-Calpine) × an energy/utility IT-spend benchmark of ~1–3% of revenue (lower than the 4–7% financial-services band used for Schwab), midpoint ~2% gives ~$454M total IT budget, discounted ~20% for the external-services subset gives ~$363M estimated annual IT services spend. That is still 10x+ the lookup table's $20M ceiling, so capped at +5 (the same workflow issue flagged on every mega-cap run of this methodology).

Realized (-4.1, Low): Geography 4 (wt. 0.10) + Revenue -5/capped-below-range (wt. 0.70) + Gross Margin -5/capped-below-range (wt. 0.20). CRM-confirmed $0 won revenue and 0% margin, re-pulled 2026-10-23 via Dataverse Web API: 0 open, 0 won, 2 closed Lost ($310.4K AI Governance 2026, $240K mobile app 2024). The July "no opportunity history" finding is superseded; the score is not, because both records are losses.

Result: High Potential / Low Realized gives SIGNIFICANT, a genuinely different placement from Schwab's POISED (High/Mid), which is a useful sanity check that the methodology discriminates between a true cold pursuit and an underleveraged-but-active account.

Strategic Priorities (Constellation, 2026)

Refreshed 2026-10-23 from the FY2025 10-K, the Q1 and Q2 2026 10-Qs, and Constellation press releases through October 2026.

  • Powering the AI and data center boom, now via bilateral deals. The 10-K names Microsoft, Google and Amazon AI investments as demand drivers. Since July the structure has clarified: Constellation signs long-term PPAs directly with hyperscalers rather than waiting on FERC co-location rules. Google (Oct 6 2026): 20-year PPA funding 890 MW of uprates across 11 units at six sites, plus a 15-year 2,700 MW PJM supply agreement, framed as $4.3B of new investment. Amazon (Sep 30 2026): 20-year Calvert Cliffs agreement for 690 MW including a 190 MW uprate, underpinning $3B+ in Maryland investment. Walmart (Jun 2026): 176 MW at Dresden. Plus 920 MW of additional PPAs signed in Q2 and the CyrusOne Texas campus deals. Microsoft Three Mile Island and Meta Clinton remain the earlier flagships; Crane restart now points to 2027.
  • Data economy is a named business. A Senior Executive Vice President, Finance and Data Economy post reporting to the CEO, held by Daniel L. Eggers and announced in November 2025. Note the correction: the earlier plan recorded this as an SVP role, which was wrong. He "leads Constellation's data economy business," building data-derived products and new value streams for the retail base. This is the cleanest services fit on the board.
  • AI in the operating core, with Google as the platform partner. Under an expanded five-year alliance Constellation "selected Google Cloud and Gemini Enterprise" and will build Gemini Enterprise agentic workflows "directly into its core operations." Three areas: speed to power (siting, power flow modeling, interconnection), generation optimization (asset health, outage management), and OT security. First primary-source confirmation of the AI platform direction.
  • Calpine integration is late-stage but not finished. $21.8B of consideration transferred, roughly $26.6B enterprise value, 55 GW combined fleet. The Q2 2026 10-Q confirms design, restructuring and integration activities were still ongoing at June 30 and that $11.1B of goodwill is only provisionally allocated. DOJ divestitures are closing: Brazos Valley ($860M) closed Oct 14; roughly 4.4 GW of PJM gas assets expected by year-end.
  • C&I digital differentiation, now with offsite renewables. Constellation Navigator pairs software with advisors for emissions and sustainability tracking. About 2.5M customer accounts including 80% of the Fortune 100. The CORe product now fronts third-party projects, for example the 15-year Toyota agreement backed by a 115 MW PPA with Ferrovial's Milano Solar in Milam County, Texas. >32% direct C&I share, 78%/88% renewal rates.
  • Workforce skills remain a named strategic pillar and a named risk factor. Unchanged.
  • Technology leadership sits under the CAO. Mike Koehler (EVP & CAO) owns IT, strategic technology platforms, cybersecurity and digital strategy. Confirmed in seat October 2026.

Market context, inference not a stated priority: on Oct 12 2026 FERC rejected most of PJM's large-load interconnection and curtailment plan while approving the data center registry. That removes the tariff path for co-located load and makes bilateral bring-your-own-power structures like the Google and Amazon deals the practical route to market, which favors Constellation.
