## Tenet Healthcare's Stated Strategic Priorities

Sourced from the FY2024 10-K, Q1 2026 and Q4/FY2025 earnings materials, the February 2026 Conifer 8-K/press release, and the April 2026 CIO-transition 8-K. Four themes.

### 1. AI and Automation at Scale, Anchored at Conifer
Tenet regained full ownership of Conifer Health Solutions specifically to expand investment in AI, automation, and global operating capabilities beyond what a joint-venture structure with CommonSpirit allowed. Management described a "green light/red light" governance model to rapidly scale effective AI pilots (ambient scribes, autonomous coding) and kill ineffective ones, and announced a partnership with Commure to deploy an ambient AI documentation platform across the employed-physician network (Tenet Physician Resources). Conifer separately partnered with Google Cloud (announced November 2025) to build a large-scale AI platform spanning patient access, eligibility verification, revenue integrity, and accounts-receivable management across more than 17 million annual patient interactions and $32B in net patient revenue.
Sources: [Press release, Feb 2, 2026 (SEC 8-K exhibit)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/70318/000007031826000003/thc-20260202exh991.htm); [Conifer investment push will go beyond AI, Tenet CEO says — Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/finance/conifer-investment-push-will-go-beyond-ai-tenet-ceo-says/); [Commure to deploy ambient AI platform for Tenet Physician Resources](https://www.commure.com/press-releases/commure-to-partner-with-tenet-healthcare-on-enterprise-wide-ai-deployment); [Conifer Redefines the Future of AI-Driven Revenue Cycle Management, Using Google Cloud](https://coniferhealth.com/press-release/conifer-redefines-the-future-of-ai-driven-revenue-cycle-management-using-google-cloud/)

### 2. Ambulatory-First Growth (USPI as the Growth Engine)
The 10-K states continued focus on expanding the Ambulatory Care segment through M&A, organic physician-relationship growth, new outpatient centers, and strategic partnerships. As of 12/31/2025, USPI held interests in 533 ambulatory surgery centers (401 consolidated) and 26 surgical hospitals (8 consolidated) across 37 states. Capital allocation priorities include a baseline $250M annually for ambulatory M&A and de novo investment.
Sources: [Tenet FY2024 10-K (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/70318/000007031825000009/thc-20241231.htm); [Tenet Reports Strong Fourth Quarter and FY 2025 Results; Provides 2026 Financial Outlook](https://investor.tenethealth.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2026/Tenet-Reports-Strong-Fourth-Quarter-and-FY-2025-Results-Provides-2026-Financial-Outlook/default.aspx)

### 3. Cost Discipline, Payer Mix, and Technology-Enabled Revenue Cycle
Q1 2026 results beat consensus on disciplined expense control (labor and supplies) combined with AI-driven productivity tools in both clinical and administrative functions. Management continues to cite investment in technology to support revenue-cycle efficiency and payer-mix improvement as an operating priority alongside the AI/automation push at Conifer.
Sources: [Earnings call transcript: Tenet Healthcare posts strong Q1 2026 EPS beat](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-tenet-healthcare-posts-strong-q1-2026-eps-beat-93CH-4674635); [Tenet Healthcare Q1 2026 slides: EPS beats amid deleveraging push](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/tenet-healthcare-q1-2026-slides-eps-beats-amid-deleveraging-push-93CH-4674638)

Lisa Y. Foo was promoted to the newly created role of EVP & Chief Operating Officer effective May 30, 2025. As COO she oversees hospital operations and leads several enterprise functions including strategy, business development, marketing, procurement, and **data, artificial intelligence, and analytics** — a structural signal that data/AI ownership is being consolidated at the COO level, distinct from and alongside the CIO's infrastructure/delivery mandate. This matters directly for this pursuit: it means the economic sponsorship for AI initiatives may sit as much with Foo's office as with the (soon-departing) CIO's.
