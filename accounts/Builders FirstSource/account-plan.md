## Builders FirstSource's Stated Strategic Priorities

Sourced from Q1 2026 earnings materials, the February 26, 2026 Blitzy partnership press release, NAHB International Builders' Show announcements, and a CIO-level podcast interview with the executive who owns this initiative. Builders FirstSource is public (NYSE: BLDR) but detailed technology-spend figures were not found broken out in its 10-K; the sourcing below relies on investor materials, press releases, and direct executive statements rather than 10-K line items.

### 1. AI-Native Engineering Velocity
The company's most concrete, dated, quantified priority. Via the Blitzy partnership (announced 2/26/2026), BFS reports a 3-5x increase in engineering development velocity and 120 engineers trained on AI-native workflows in the first three months. President of Technology & Digital Solutions Gayatri Narayan describes the underlying strategy directly: "We've seen a three to four times increase in engineering velocity — especially in refactoring legacy systems where historically there was very little knowledge of how the system actually worked," and frames AI as a modernization enabler rather than a replacement strategy: "companies that have existed for 20 or 30 years don't have to get bogged down by legacy stacks."
Sources: [Blitzy Accelerates Software Development 3x with Leading Building Materials Supplier — PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/blitzy-accelerates-software-development-3x-with-leading-building-materials-supplier-302698225.html); [Builders FirstSource Deploys AI Development Platform to Speed Digital Projects — Distribution Strategy Group](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/03/builders-firstsource-deploys-ai-development-platform-to-speed-digital-projects/); [From Technical Debt to 4x Engineering Velocity with Gayatri Narayan of Builders FirstSource — CIO Pod](https://ciopod.com/podcasts/from-technical-debt-to-4x-engineering-velocity-with-gayatri-narayan-of-builders-firstsource/)

### 2. Next-Generation Digital Solutions Platform (myBLDR.com)
Debuted at the NAHB International Builders' Show: a next-generation version of myBLDR.com with four integrated hubs (community, plan, selections, construction), embedded AI providing "actionable insights through a single unified platform," designed to address disconnection across design, purchasing, sales, and construction processes. Targeted for H2 2026 launch. The original myBLDR.com platform launched February 2024 and has processed more than $1.5B in orders since; management has reaffirmed a full-year goal of $334M in incremental digital revenue.
Sources: [Builders FirstSource Deploys AI Development Platform to Speed Digital Projects — Distribution Strategy Group](https://distributionstrategy.com/2026/03/builders-firstsource-deploys-ai-development-platform-to-speed-digital-projects/); [Builders FirstSource grows Q1 digital sales to $153 million — Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/05/02/builders-firstsource-digital-sales-q1-fy25/); [myBLDR — Your Digital Homebuilding Platform](https://www.bldr.com/digital-tools/mybldr)

### 3. Operational Excellence and Cost Discipline (the "Downturn Playbook")
Q1 2026: net sales down 10.1% YoY to $3.29B (8.3% core organic decline, 3.3% commodity deflation), net loss of $47.4M vs. net income of $96.3M a year earlier. Management response: 21 facility consolidations in 2026, deeper cuts to overtime and temporary labor, reduced merit/overhead spending, tighter discretionary spend controls. Operational excellence initiatives delivered $6M in Q1 productivity savings via supply chain/logistics optimization. This is the budget-pressure backdrop against which every technology investment decision is now being made — and it is precisely why an AI-driven engineering-capacity story (doing more without adding headcount) is landing internally right now.
Sources: [Builders FirstSource Q1 2026 slides: earnings miss amid housing slump — Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/builders-firstsource-q1-2026-slides-earnings-miss-amid-housing-slump-93CH-4650421); [Builders FirstSource Q1 sales fall 10%, posts loss — StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BLDR/builders-first-source-reports-first-quarter-2026-m1fv10ctw6sp.html)

41 acquisitions completed since the 2021 BMC merger, including one in Q1 2026 ($12M, establishing truss operations in New York). **Entry pitch:** "I know the current environment has you tightening spend everywhere — facility consolidations, overtime cuts, discretionary spend controls.
