## 2. What Expedia has told the market it cares about

The four themes below are sourced from Expedia's FY2025 10-K and the Q2 2026 (Aug 5, 2026) earnings call, a full primary-source trail since Expedia is a standard NASDAQ filer. The 2026-10-23 refresh subsection that follows is sourced differently: the company's own press releases plus trade and business press quoting executives directly. Each claim there carries its own citation and its own confidence tag.

**AI-powered traveler experience is the current core thesis.** CEO Ariane Gorin, on the Q2 2026 call, described AI deployed across search, recommendations, trip-planning personalization, and faster internal engineering velocity, calling out "immediate impact." Expedia expanded partnerships with ChatGPT and Google and acquired AI travel-planning app Layla to reach travelers earlier in the booking funnel. On Vrbo, an agentic voice solution now handles partner inquiries previously done by human agents.

**Platform consolidation is the technical foundation underneath everything else.** The FY2025 10-K states Expedia migrated both Hotels.com and Vrbo onto the unified Brand Expedia front-end stack (completed 2023), explicitly to "increase test-and-learn capacity and feature release velocity."

**One Key unified loyalty is a stated relationship priority.** Cross-brand loyalty (Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo) rolled out in the UK in 2024; in 2025 most legacy Expedia Rewards members migrated onto it — a stated move toward direct, durable traveler relationships rather than transactional bookings.

**B2B platform growth is the fastest-growing, most quietly under-the-radar line of business.** CFO Derek Andersen, on the Q2 2026 call, described building "a one-stop travel platform for partners." B2B posted its 20th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth (bookings +12% YoY to $33.9B, revenue +14% to $4.3B).

### What changed since the August 2026 draft (added 2026-10-23)

**AI-first operating model with data as the stated moat.** Xavier Amatriain, Chief Data and AI Officer (first holder of the role, in seat since late 2025, previously at Google), describes Expedia as pursuing an "AI-first" operating model and calls data "a differentiator and a moat for us." He runs a fan-out/fan-in method: let teams test many tools, models, and agents, then concentrate resources on what proves out, while keeping at least 80% of staff on a "golden path" and using designated "AI champions" as internal evangelists. He favors small "mission complete" squads with few cross-team dependencies and blurry boundaries between engineers, product managers, and scientists. On models he is explicitly vendor-agnostic: "We use frontier, proprietary models, but we also use open weight models," arguing that "models are going to be fungible" and "You don't need to get hooked into any specific model." *Source: CIO Dive, 2026-10-16.*

**Distribution through third-party AI agents is now the core AI bet, not a single Expedia-owned agent.** Expedia is now bookable inside Meta's Muse, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT, Claude, and Alexa. It abandoned a single end-to-end trip agent in favor of specialized "point agents" in a multi-agent architecture. CEO Ariane Gorin told Skift she does "not believe that a large portion of bookings are going to be an end-to-end conversational experience," and said AI tools are not yet lifting bookings but do return "more than 60% more" information on traveler intent, higher engagement, and fewer cancellations among users who then book. Answer engine optimization is now Expedia's fastest-growing channel. The Meta Muse integration was announced around 2026-09-22 and is the most consequential single change in this refresh. *Sources: Skift 2026-09-23; Seeking Alpha 2026-09-22; 24/7 Wall St. 2026-10-01.*

**Autonomous Onboarding (new product, 2026-10-20).** A launch that lets properties join the Expedia marketplace through existing connectivity software and go live "within 24 hours," automating property data transfer and validation for rates, photos, amenities, and availability, and removing duplicate data entry. Expedia positions it as giving properties access to its B2B network of "over 75,000 partners and 200,000 travel advisors" and to "AI-powered travel partners." HotelRunner was named as an integrating partner. This is an automation and data-pipeline product, not a marketing story. *Sources: Expedia Group release 2026-10-20, via Yahoo Finance and PYMNTS.*

**Largest-ever vacation rental launch (2026-09-01).** Twelve new partner products across Vrbo and Escapia, including Vrbo Sponsored Listings, Same-Day Bookings, a modernized Escapia Reservation Grid, Distribution Optimization, and a Vrbo Payments pilot that connects to One Key rewards. Executives quoted: Tim Rosolio (VP, Vacation Rental Partnerships) and Joel Schoen (GM, Escapia). *Source: Expedia Group release 2026-09-01.*

**One Key revamp (2026-10-06).** One Key added airport lounge access during flight delays and an expedited security benefit. This follows a July 2026 change that scaled back benefits for casual travelers, so the program is being re-tiered toward higher-value, higher-frequency members. *Sources: Expedia release 2026-10-06; Forbes 2026-07-30.*

**Vrbo economics are being reset, effective 2026-10-29.** Vrbo is moving all hosts to a single 12% commission, replacing a 5% rate for PMS-connected property managers and 8% for non-connected hosts, and retiring a legacy subscription plan. The new terms reportedly add rate parity and permit Vrbo to distribute rates through AI agents and metasearch. There was visible host blowback and a partial retreat: Skift reported on 2026-10-19 that some properties, including some Escapia users, were exempted from losing their subscription plans. **Treat the commission and rate-parity specifics as needs-verification trade press, and treat the direction as confirmed.** What matters for Improving is that this is a pricing and distribution-systems change with a hard date attached, which means engineering scope.

**Autonomous Onboarding is phase one of a bigger stated program.** Expedia describes it as the first phase of an "Autonomous Distribution" vision. Five connectivity providers shaped the pilot: HotelRunner, Yanolja Cloud Solutions, RoomCloud, RateGain, and Eviivo. That is a live, named integration surface.

**No change to the four themes above.** AI-powered traveler experience, platform consolidation, One Key, and B2B growth all remain live and citable. The August and October changes extend them rather than replace them.

**Note on the CRM revenue field:** the CRM record shows Annual Revenue as $1,000,000,000 — this is a stale placeholder, not Expedia's real figure. Actual FY2025 revenue is **$14.7 billion**. This has been corrected in the 9-box calculation below and should be corrected in the CRM record.
