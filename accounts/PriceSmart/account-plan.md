## PriceSmart's Stated Strategic Priorities

Sourced from PriceSmart's FY2025 Form 10-K (filed October 30, 2025), fiscal Q2 2026 and Q3 2026 earnings releases (April 8, 2026 and July 8, 2026), and PriceSmart's own investor relations materials. Five themes:

### 1. Regional Expansion — New Country Entry and Club Growth
PriceSmart is opening its first Chilean location (Mallplaza Los Dominicos, Santiago — its first-ever mall-based club format) with a targeted spring 2027 opening, alongside an 11th Costa Rica club and an 8th Guatemala club (Villa Nueva), bringing the planned network to 61 total clubs. As of May 31, 2026 the company operated 57 clubs in 12 countries plus one U.S. territory.
Sources: [PriceSmart Q3 FY2026 Operating Results — StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/PSMT/pricesmart-announces-fiscal-2026-third-quarter-operating-results-fggkq04n9yro.html); [PriceSmart Announces Fiscal 2026 Second Quarter Operating Results and Plans for Eighth Club in Guatemala — PriceSmart IR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pricesmart-announces-fiscal-2026-second-quarter-operating-results-and-plans-for-eighth-club-in-guatemala-302737412.html)

### 2. Digital & Omnichannel Growth
Digital channel sales reached $306.7 million in FY2025 (+21.6% YoY, 6.0% of net merchandise sales), and quarterly digital sales hit a record $99.6 million in Q3 FY2026 (+26.2% YoY), with omnichannel now 6.9% of net merchandise sales. The FY2025 10-K states the company will "begin migrating our mobile application to fully native iOS and Android architectures to enhance speed, reliability, and accessibility" during FY2026.
Sources: [PriceSmart FY2025 10-K (filed 10/30/2025) — StockTitan filing analysis](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/PSMT/10-k-pricesmart-inc-files-annual-report-b934e5e08d77.html); [PriceSmart Q3 FY2026 Operating Results — StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/PSMT/pricesmart-announces-fiscal-2026-third-quarter-operating-results-fggkq04n9yro.html)

### 3. Supply Chain and POS Technology Modernization
The FY2025 10-K describes substantial progress migrating to the **RELEX** supply chain platform, with full implementation targeted for FY2026, stated to "enhance employee productivity" and "improve inventory management, reduce spoilage and increase in-stock availability, driving both sales and efficiency." In parallel, PriceSmart is rolling out the **Elera** point-of-sale system across its English-speaking Caribbean markets (Q1 FY2026) and Spanish-speaking Central American markets (through FY2026), alongside new distribution-center capacity in China and multi-club markets.
Source: [PriceSmart FY2025 10-K (filed 10/30/2025) — StockTitan filing analysis](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/PSMT/10-k-pricesmart-inc-files-annual-report-b934e5e08d77.html)

### 4. Private Label Growth ("Member's Selection")
Member's Selection private-label products reached 28.1% of total merchandise sales in FY2025, up from 27.6% in FY2024, with stated plans for continued investment in additional private-label products under the brand.
Source: [PriceSmart FY2025 10-K (filed 10/30/2025) — StockTitan filing analysis](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/PSMT/10-k-pricesmart-inc-files-annual-report-b934e5e08d77.html)

### 5. Membership Growth and Retention
Membership reached 2.08 million as of Q2 FY2026 with a record renewal rate of 90.2% (as of February 28, 2026), and membership income rose 16.9% YoY to $33.2 million in Q2 FY2026. Full-year FY2025 membership was 2.01 million accounts, up from 1.89 million the prior year.
Sources: [PriceSmart 2026 Outlook — Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/pricesmarts-2026-outlook-scaling-regional-195353988.html); [PriceSmart Q2 FY2026 Investor Presentation — PriceSmart IR](https://investors.pricesmart.com/events-and-presentations/presentations/presentation-details/2026/Q2-2026-Investor-Presentation-2026-c4AO3MTbG3/default.aspx)
