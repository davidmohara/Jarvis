Recommended entry play · Option A

Data Platform Enablement on the AWS + Snowflake AI Layer

Position Improving's Data Enablement Engagement on the specific AWS/Snowflake data layer underlying Marriott's confirmed AI pricing, personalization, distribution and analytics work. A scoped, fixed-price entry point rather than competing for the whole PMS/CRS/loyalty transformation.
“You're scaling AI-driven pricing, personalization and now distribution content across AWS and Snowflake, across a portfolio that just passed 10,000 properties and 295 million loyalty members. We run a fixed-scope, 4-to-8-week data enablement engagement that builds one prioritized slice of that data platform properly and hands your team a repeatable pattern. Where's the data engineering constraint slowing that down right now?”
Grounding (offering gate, 2026-10-23): real, priced offering: Data Enablement Engagement, 4–8 weeks at $125K–$250K (Data Enablement Engagement.docx, Sales Offerings tree, pulled 10/23). The prior "embedded delivery pod" framing mapped to a draft, unapproved offering that excludes Fortune-500-scale accounts, so the play was reworded. Why lead here: It's grounded in two confirmed, named technology dependencies (AWS, Snowflake) and a named, priced offering rather than a generic pitch into a $1.25–1.35B program where Improving has no footprint, no warm relationship, and a real chance a large incumbent SI already owns the core replatform. Refreshed 10/23/2026: the addressable data/AI surface grew with the guidance increase and the 295M-member Bonvoy base, and Prachi Priya now gives this play a named, plausible working-level sponsor.
