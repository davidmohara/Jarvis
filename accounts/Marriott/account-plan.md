Disambiguation note: "Marriott" is a common brand name attached to thousands of individually franchised/managed properties worldwide — this plan anchors specifically on the publicly traded parent corporate entity (Marriott International, Inc.), confirmed via ticker (MAR) and HQ address, not any single franchised hotel or the related-but-distinct Marriott Vacations Worldwide (NYSE: VAC, a separate public company spun off in 2011 that runs the timeshare business — do not conflate the two when researching leadership, e.g. a General Counsel transition news item surfaced during research belongs to Marriott Vacations Worldwide, not Marriott International, and was excluded on that basis).

**Engagement shape: COLD PURSUIT — CRM-CONFIRMED (8/7/2026, live Dynamics pull).** A live Dynamics CRM search for "Marriott" found no corporate account for Marriott International, Inc. at all. What exists in CRM is unrelated: **Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation** (a separate, publicly traded company, NYSE: VAC, spun off from Marriott International in 2011 — owner Shalini Arora, zero open opportunities, no closed-revenue history in its own AI-generated account summary) and individual franchise-property lead records (**Courtyard by Marriott: Foster City**, owner Jeff Dodds, zero open opportunities; **Washington Dulles Airport Marriott and Suites**, not opened in detail but same pattern) — these are independently owned/managed hotel properties, not the corporate parent, and show no meaningful engagement history either. No internal Improving document, account folder, or CRM record references any Marriott International business relationship. This confirms — not just infers — that this is a genuine cold, new-logo pursuit.

**Timing trigger.** Two stacked, dated, high-confidence signals:
1. **A disclosed $1.1 billion 2026 investment spending commitment, with more than one-third directed to digital and technology transformation** — specifically replatforming three core systems: the property management system, central reservations infrastructure, and the Marriott Bonvoy loyalty platform, onto a new cloud-native architecture. Global CIO Naveen Manga has publicly framed 2026 as "a year for scale" in this multiyear transformation. (Source: Q4/FY2025 earnings materials and CIO Dive/Hotel Technology News coverage, February 2026.)
2. **Active, named AI deployment across the enterprise** — natural language search shipping to marriott.com and the Bonvoy app in H1 2026, an "Ask Bonvoy" AI-powered search experience launched June 16, 2026, and named collaborations with Google and OpenAI. More than 1,000 hotels have already transitioned to the new tech ecosystem as of Q1 2026, with AI-driven dynamic pricing contributing to a 6.2% YoY RevPAR increase. (Sources: Marriott press release, news.marriott.com, June 16, 2026; CIO Dive, February 2026.)

The account's real story: a company in the middle of one of the largest technology re-platforming efforts in the hospitality industry — PMS, CRS, and loyalty all being rebuilt simultaneously on cloud-native infrastructure — with a CIO publicly describing 2026 as the year execution has to scale, and apparently zero existing Improving footprint to build from. This is a pure new-logo pursuit into a company already spending at a scale that dwarfs a typical account.

## Entry Plays / Opportunity Scenarios

### Option A — Data/AI Platform Delivery Pod (AWS + Snowflake)

Position Improving as embedded delivery capacity inside the specific AWS/Snowflake data layer underlying Marriott's confirmed AI pricing, personalization, and analytics work — a scoped, credible entry point rather than competing for the whole PMS/CRS/loyalty transformation.

**Entry pitch:** "You're scaling AI-driven pricing and personalization on AWS and Snowflake across an 8,000+ hotel portfolio. Where's the data engineering capacity constraint slowing that down right now?"

### Option B — Loyalty Platform Engineering Surge

Target the Bonvoy loyalty replatform specifically — a named, dated initiative (H1 2026 natural-language search, June 2026 "Ask Bonvoy" launch) with a ~271M-member scale that Improving's product engineering practice is built to support.

**Entry pitch:** "You've just shipped Ask Bonvoy to 271 million members. What's the next capability on the loyalty roadmap that needs senior product engineering capacity faster than a new-vendor onboarding cycle allows?"

### Option C — Transformation-Program Assessment

A short, paid discovery engagement assessing a specific slice of the PMS/CRS/loyalty replatform's technical risk or delivery pace — lower commitment, but requires executive access Improving doesn't currently have and competes directly with whatever large SI/hyperscaler professional-services team is very plausibly already engaged on the core program.

**Entry pitch:** "A $1.1B technology transformation program at your scale always has at least one workstream running behind the others. A short, focused assessment could surface where that is and what unblocks it."

**Recommendation:** Lead with **Option A**. It is the most credibly scoped entry — grounded in two confirmed, named technology dependencies (AWS, Snowflake) rather than a generic pitch into a $1.1B program where Improving has no existing footprint, no warm relationship, and a real chance a large incumbent SI already owns the core replatform. Option B is a credible second move once any relationship exists. Option C is aspirational and gated on executive access this plan doesn't have.
