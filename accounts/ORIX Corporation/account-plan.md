## 1. Read this first: the entity and the real starting point

The target is **ORIX Corporation USA** ("ORIX USA"), headquartered at 2001 Ross Avenue, Trammell Crow Center, Dallas, TX — a wholly-owned US subsidiary of Tokyo/NYSE-listed **ORIX Corporation** (TSE: 8591, NYSE: IX), with reported assets over $5 billion and ~1,200 employees across corporate finance, real estate capital, municipal finance, asset management, and specialty finance lines.

**This is not a new pursuit.** Improving has been an active vendor to ORIX USA since at least **December 2023** (CRM customer record dates to 9/20/2021, WorkDay Customer ID C-0798). Direct CRM query confirms:

- **$3,267,720 in closed-won revenue** across **17 of 28** total opportunities (61% win rate) since 12/18/2023.
- **Zero currently open opportunities** — every deal on record has already closed won, lost ("Out-Sold" — lost to a competing vendor), or been cancelled by the client ("Project Canceled").
- **Trailing-12-month won revenue ≈ $918,800** (5 deals closing between 7/30/2025 and 8/6/2026) — right at the account-planning methodology's $1M scoring boundary.
- **Recent momentum is strong:** three deals worth $520,800 combined closed in the three weeks between 7/23/2026 and 8/6/2026 alone.
- **34 contacts and 21 logged email threads** on the account — this is a real, worked relationship, not a name in a spreadsheet.

**What the engagement actually is:** every closed-won deal is an **individual staff-augmentation placement or contract extension** — a Business Systems Analyst, a Project Manager, a T-SQL/SQL Developer, an HR PM, named consultants like "Deepika Ragipalli" and "Monika Gupta" being extended on assignment. There is no platform, data, or AI engagement on record. This is the textbook **active-but-underleveraged** pattern (the Schwab pattern): real access and a strong win rate, concentrated entirely in one tactical service category.

**The opening:** ORIX USA already trusts Improving to staff individual roles reliably and extend them repeatedly. The expansion path is proving a larger, higher-margin engagement (a small platform/data pod, an assessment, an embedded team) rather than one more individual placement — using the existing win rate and relationship as the credibility base, not starting from zero.
