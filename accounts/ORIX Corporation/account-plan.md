**Global reorganization into regional and digital units.** ORIX Corp's Dec 10, 2025 6-K confirms a full structural reorg effective Jan/April 2026, including a dedicated **USA & Europe Business Unit** and a new **Digital Innovation Unit** (infosec + technology + a new Operational Reform Department). **"PATHWAYS" growth strategy — AI infrastructure, digital transformation, BPaaS.** ORIX Group's 2025 IR materials name PATHWAYS as a core focus under "Growth Strategy 2035" (targeting 15% ROE / ¥1T net income by FY2035), citing investment in IT/AI infrastructure, digital transformation, and Business Process as a Service.

### 2a. Refresh, October 2026: what the newest primary sources add

Three 2026 primary sources materially sharpen this section: ORIX Group's **Integrated Report 2026** (published October 2026), the **Q1 FY2027.3 results presentation** (August 6, 2026), and ORIX USA's own **leadership page** (retrieved October 2026).

**The group has repositioned around a hybrid asset-management model.** The CFO & CSO message frames the shift explicitly: move away from relying on balance-sheet expansion, earn investment income from proprietary holdings, and grow fee income from third-party capital. Management cites an AUM-to-total-assets ratio of 0.4x (roughly ¥7tn private-asset AUM against ¥18tn of total assets) as too low versus global peers and names closing that gap as the core financial task. **The single most useful line for Improving sits in that same message.** The supporting investments listed for the hybrid model include "upgraded CRM, reporting, and middle/back-office systems." **Two narrowing caveats worth carrying.** First, the USA & Europe Business COO message lists five priorities (integrate client bases and distribution, build joint products for institutional investors, share investment knowledge, extend US private-asset/ABL/real-estate strategies into Europe, use US networks to support European growth) and names **no** digital, technology, or AI initiative. PATHWAYS still names IT and AI infrastructure at group level (and adds space and satellites), but the 2026 USA-specific messaging is quieter on technology as a growth sector and louder on operations, systems, and fee income. **Q1 FY2027.3 (quarter ended June 30, 2026, announced August 6, 2026).** Net income was ¥280.8B, up 162% year on year, a record quarter and 53% of the full-year forecast. The **USA & Europe segment posted ¥63.0B of profit, up 494%**, driven by fair value gains on ORIX USA private equity investments plus European asset-management fee growth. Segment assets rose to ¥2.86T with ROA of 7.0%, up from ¥2.74T, on expanded lending at NXT and growth in asset-based lending. The same presentation introduces a three-category matrix (Finance / Operation / Investments) effective FY2027.3, in which ORIX USA appears three times: the financing business under Finance, Hilco Global under Operation, and private equity under Investments.

**Budget pressure, not just budget.** The Integrated Report discloses that US profit declined significantly in the fiscal year ended March 2026, attributed to portfolio optimization and impairments on legacy assets, with several private equity holdings sold as part of capital recycling. *Reasoned inference, flagged:* profit is being repaired through exits and fair-value gains rather than through operating expansion, which argues for leading with a tightly scoped, costed assessment rather than an open-ended platform program.

**Leadership correction.** The Integrated Report 2026 CEO message dates Hidetake Takahashi's appointment as President and Group COO to January 2025, with Group CEO following later. An earlier draft of this plan dated it January 1, 2026; the primary source supersedes it.
