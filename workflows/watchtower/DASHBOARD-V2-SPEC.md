## FUTURE — Proprietary Analytical Lens + Thesis Alignment (PARKED, not building now)

**Status:** David wants this LATER, not now (his words, 2026-06-19: "I don't want it now but I will"). Do NOT build until David explicitly says go. This section captures the intent so it's ready when he calls for it.

**The idea.** Rex Miller maps every signal onto his proprietary "Money Matrix" — a consistent analytical frame that turns a pile of news into a worldview. The lesson is not to copy his matrix; it's to run every Watchtower signal through DAVID's OWN proprietary lens so the dashboard expresses a coherent thesis rather than just summarizing news. This is the move that turns Watchtower from a digest into a point of view.

### Component 1 — The Lens (per-item mapping)
Each surfaced item gets mapped onto David's strategic frame(s). Candidate frames to draw from (David picks/refines the canonical set when we build):
- **Extraction vs. Leverage** — is this development about cutting/replacing, or about redeploying human capability? (Borrowed framing, but central to David's view of AI in services.)
- **Build vs. Buy vs. Partner** — where does this push the mid-market CIO decision?
- **The CAIO Execution Playbook** — David's existing framework (`reference/CAIO-Execution-Playbook.md`); map items to its stages.
- **Container vs. Contents / commoditization curve** — what's becoming infrastructure vs. where the durable margin moves.
- **Relationship/trust/regional-presence moat** — the consulting counter-position to lab-led delivery.
David supplies the canonical lens set at build time. The run tags each item with which frame(s) it lands on and a one-line "where it sits on the frame."

### Component 2 — Thesis Alignment (daily/weekly synthesis)
A recurring scored judgment of how the day's/week's signals align with — or challenge — David's standing thesis. Two parts:
- A short statement of David's current thesis (sourced from identity/GOALS_AND_DREAMS.md, the CAIO playbook, and his content) — e.g. "AI labs commoditize the model; durable value moves to governed, relationship-led delivery."
- A daily "alignment read": do today's signals REINFORCE the thesis, COMPLICATE it, or CONTRADICT it? Surface contradicting signals loudly — the system earns its keep by flagging when the world is arguing with David's worldview, not just confirming it. Avoid an echo chamber.

### Where it would render
A dashboard tab or panel ("Lens" / "Thesis"): the day's items plotted on the frame, plus the alignment read (reinforce / complicate / contradict) with the contradicting items called out.

### Data this would require (future step changes)
- A canonical lens definition file (e.g. `workflows/watchtower/lens.yaml`) holding David's frames + current thesis statement, editable by him.
- `daily-step-03-score.md` (or a new lens sub-step) extended to tag each item with frame mapping.
- A new synthesis sub-step producing the daily `thesis_alignment` field.
- Dashboard v3 to render the Lens/Thesis panel.

### Guardrails when we build it
- It's DAVID's lens, defined by David — Master/Knox do not invent the frames. Pull the canonical set from David at build time.
- Must surface disconfirming signals, not just confirmation. Explicitly anti-echo-chamber.
- Still no copying of Rex's Money Matrix structure or labels — different frames, David's IP.
- Internal tool, not client-facing; no improving-brand styling.
