The Agent Got Its Own Email Address
AI Identity ►
New argument from W41 — agent becomes first-class org member, not a tool
Google’s Gemini universal agent (Oct 8, Gemini at Work 2026) gives enterprise agents their own Workspace identity: email, calendar, Drive, OAuth scope, audit trail. W41 was about governance as a product category; W42’s argument is that agents are now first-class organizational members. The governance question shifts from “how do we audit agent access?” to “how do we provision agents into our directory?” For Microsoft-stack IT teams: agent identity in Entra ID is the decision happening this quarter.

The 5 Percent Problem
AI Consulting ►
New argument from W41 — identifies what the firms closing the gap actually do
McKinsey 2026 State of AI: 37% of orgs report AI contributed to EBIT — unchanged YoY despite investment set to double. PwC CEO Survey: 56% saw neither revenue growth nor cost reduction. Only 5–8% show measurable P&L impact at scale. What they do differently: embed AI in high-volume workflows with clean before/after metrics, redesign roles rather than layer AI on top, and measure rigorously. W41 measured the readiness gap; W42 identifies what the 5% who close it actually do.

Texas AI ►
