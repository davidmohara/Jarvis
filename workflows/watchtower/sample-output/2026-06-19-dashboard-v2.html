What It Means

Two of the largest AI labs — Anthropic and OpenAI — launched enterprise delivery arms within weeks of each other, backed by a combined $5.5B in capital. Taken alongside Microsoft's Build 2026 agentic stack (MAF, Work IQ APIs, Scout) going GA, the infrastructure for AI-native delivery is being commoditized from the top down in real time. This is not a slow-moving structural shift; the capital is already deployed and the teams are already recruiting from the same talent pool as regional consulting firms.

The governance signal cuts the other direction. Gartner's 40%/40% prediction and the Agentic AI Institute's 60% governance-gap finding define the market condition that keeps consulting firms relevant: clients need delivery guidance, not just tooling. The firms that get ahead of the first major governance failure — with a repeatable playbook, not a capabilities deck — will own the remediation and advisory work when the inevitable headline arrives. That's the opening that's available right now, and it plays to regional depth rather than global scale.
