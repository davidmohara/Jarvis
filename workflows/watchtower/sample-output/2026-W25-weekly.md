# Watchtower — 2026-W25

## Themes This Week

### 1. AI Labs Are Moving Into the Services Layer

Anthropic ($1.5B, Blackstone/Goldman) and OpenAI ($4B, TPG, Tomoro acquisition) both launched enterprise consulting/delivery arms within weeks of each other. This is not coincidence. The labs have identified the services margin as the next frontier and are moving on it with institutional backing. Combined with Microsoft's Build 2026 agentic stack announcements (MAF, Work IQ APIs GA, Scout), the infrastructure for AI-native delivery is being commoditized from the top down. For IT consulting firms, this is the most important competitive development in the current cycle.

**Content angles:** "The AI Labs Ate the Services Margin" (blog/LinkedIn); "Microsoft Just Handed Us the Roadmap" (blog/LinkedIn)

### 2. Governance Is the Gap and the Opportunity

Gartner's dual prediction (40% enterprise apps with agents by EOY 2026; 40%+ projects canceled by 2027) defines the exact market tension that makes consulting firms relevant. The failure mode is governance, not technology. 72% of firms have agents in production; 60% lack governance structures. The consolidation wave ($1.2T IT services M&A in 5 months, AI capability as primary acquisition target) confirms that the market is already pricing AI delivery competency as a durable asset. Firms that build governance-first agentic playbooks now will own remediation and advisory work when the failure headlines arrive.

**Content angles:** "The Governance Gap Is the Opportunity" (blog/LinkedIn/Forbes); "The Consolidation Wave and What It Means to Be Acquirable" (blog/LinkedIn/Forbes)
