# The AI Labs Ate the Services Margin

## Hook
Anthropic just raised $1.5B and launched an enterprise services firm. OpenAI did the same two weeks earlier with $4B and an acquisition. The labs aren't coming for your software budget. They're coming for your consulting budget.

## Story Angle
I run a regional practice for an IT consulting firm. We've spent the last two years helping clients figure out where AI fits in their business. That's a legitimate, billable service. Now the companies making the AI are deciding to do the delivery themselves. Blackstone and Goldman Sachs are not backing Anthropic's new venture out of intellectual curiosity. They're backing it because they see where the services margin is going and they want a seat at the table. I have to be honest with myself about what that means for firms like Improving.

## Core Insight
The real competition for IT consulting firms was never Accenture or Deloitte. It was always going to be the model vendors once they figured out the services layer was more valuable than pure software licensing. That moment just arrived. The question for every regional and mid-sized consulting firm isn't "how do we integrate AI" anymore. It's "what do we offer that the AI lab's own delivery arm cannot?"

## Challenge / CTA
What's your honest answer to that question for your firm? (I'm still working on mine.)
