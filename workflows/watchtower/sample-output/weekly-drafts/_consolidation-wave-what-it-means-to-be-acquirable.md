# The Consolidation Wave and What It Means to Be Acquirable

## Hook
$1.2 trillion in IT services M&A in five months. The buyers aren't collecting revenue. They're buying AI delivery capability that took years to build. That changes what it means to invest in your practice right now.

## Story Angle
I think about this more than I probably let on. Improving operates in the exact market segment that acquirers are hunting in right now. Generative AI delivery capability, agentic systems, platform consulting, an existing mid-market client base. We're not a large firm. That's not a liability in this market, it's a targeting profile. And I have a choice about how I think about that. I can see it as threat (we might get absorbed and lose our identity) or as validation (the capabilities we've been building for the last two years are exactly what the market is willing to pay $4B+ acquisitions to obtain). I think it's mostly the second one, but you don't get to that conclusion without sitting with the first one for a minute.

## Core Insight
The consolidation wave in IT services is a vote of confidence in AI delivery competency as a durable asset. For regional consulting firms, the strategic imperative is the same whether you're building to be acquired or building to stay independent: your AI delivery capability has to be real, repeatable, and referenceable. Acquirers and clients are both asking the same question right now.

## Challenge / CTA
If someone wrote a deal memo on your practice today, what would the AI delivery section say? Is that the section you'd be proud of?
