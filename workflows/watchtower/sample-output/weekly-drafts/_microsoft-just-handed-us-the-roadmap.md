# Microsoft Just Handed Us the Roadmap

## Hook
Every enterprise client I have runs on Microsoft. Every one of them. Build 2026 wasn't a developer conference. It was a product roadmap for the next three years of billable agentic work, and most consulting firms missed it.

## Story Angle
I watched the Build 2026 announcements drop: the Microsoft Agent Framework going open-source with MCP baked in, Work IQ APIs going GA, Scout launching as the first always-on autonomous agent. And I kept thinking the same thing. This is not a technology story. This is a services story. Every one of these launches is a new entry point for delivery work with the clients who already trust Improving. The firms that understand MAF and Work IQ at a practitioner level in the next six months are the ones who get the first calls when enterprise clients say "we want to build on this." The firms that are still writing slide decks about AI strategy in Q4 are going to be watching from the sidelines.

## Core Insight
Microsoft's agentic stack is already in the hands of Improving's clients. The question is which consulting partners get certified and credentialed on it first, and which ones show up six months late with a capabilities deck.

## Challenge / CTA
If your firm runs on Microsoft, you already have the install base. The question is whether you're treating Build 2026 like news or like a plan.
