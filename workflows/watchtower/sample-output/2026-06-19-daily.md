### [Texas Grid Tests Put AI Data Center Growth on Notice This Summer](https://startupfortune.com/texas-grid-tests-put-ai-data-center-growth-on-notice-this-summer/)
*Score: 74 | Source: Texas Tribune*

ERCOT's 2026 large-load interconnection queue has reached 226 gigawatts of requests against a grid with ~103 GW of actual capacity — AI companies represent 73% of new applications. Texas Senate Bill 6 now requires large-load users over 75 MW to share interconnection costs, and several data center facilities failed recent voltage ride-through assessments. The economic math is stark: adding projected data center demand could push average wholesale electricity prices up 49%. This is a structural constraint on Texas's AI infrastructure buildout and affects the cost calculus for hyperscalers and enterprise data center operators David's clients interact with. It's also a legitimate enterprise infrastructure risk topic for CIO conversations.
