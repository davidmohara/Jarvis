# Watchtower — 2026-W37

## Themes This Week

### 1. The CIO Is Holding the Bag
CIO Dive's reporting shows CIOs absorbing organizational blame when AI initiatives fail, even when root cause sits in business-side governance gaps or decision rights that were never assigned. This is new territory from prior governance themes: not the governance gap in the abstract but the specific career and accountability consequence landing on a named role. For David's clients, it reframes the conversation from "do you have an AI governance framework" to "who specifically owns the outcome when your agent makes the wrong call, and does your org chart reflect that."

**Content angles:** The CIO Is Holding the Bag

### 2. Adoption Is Real. Scale Is Theater.
Forrester finds most enterprises pursuing agentic AI, but almost none have scaled beyond chatbots. The 72% "agents in production" stat from Agentic AI Institute lands differently next to Forrester's "few are catching" headline: what's in production isn't what people think when they say "in production." New from W37's build-decision theme — the specific argument here is that the adoption scorecard is being falsified by how "production" is defined, and the consulting angle is the gap between headline and reality.

**Content angles:** Adoption Is Real. Scale Is Theater.
