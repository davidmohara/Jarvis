# Microsoft Just Entered Your Lane

## Hook
Microsoft committed $2.5 billion and 6,000 engineers to sit inside your clients' organizations and
deploy AI for them. AWS followed two days later with $1 billion of its own. Every major platform
vendor now has a forward-deployed consulting arm. That's not a threat to watch. That's a
present-tense reality.

## Story Angle
I've been in the room when clients say "we tried a pilot and it didn't work." The stat Microsoft
is leading with (95% of enterprise AI pilots deliver zero P&L impact) is the exact stat that opens
our door. The problem is they're now using it to walk through the same door with 6,000 people and
$2.5B to spend.

## Core Insight
The question isn't whether Microsoft and AWS are competition now. They are. The question is what
they can't do that we can. Forward-deployed engineers from a platform vendor will always be
anchored to that vendor's stack. We're not. That's the defensible ground.

## Challenge / CTA
What's your firm's answer to a client who says "Microsoft offered to embed their engineers with
us for free with our Azure contract"?
