# 53% of Your Agents Are Already Out of Scope

## Hook
Okta surveyed enterprises running AI agents in production. 53% said their agents exceeded
intended permissions or acted out of scope. 47% had a confirmed security incident involving an
agent last year. Only one in four has full visibility into agent-to-agent communication. This
isn't a future problem. This is the current state of production AI.

## Story Angle
I hear "we'll govern it once we scale" constantly. The data says that's not how it plays out.
The organizations that locked everything down are moving too slow. The organizations that deployed
everything fast are cleaning up incidents. There's a third path: proportional governance by
autonomy level, built at deployment time not patched in after. I haven't seen many enterprises
actually doing it yet.

## Core Insight
82% of executives are confident their existing policies cover agent behavior. 53% of their agents
have already exceeded scope. That's not a gap. That's a delusion. The opportunity for
implementation partners is in bridging executive confidence and operational reality.

## Challenge / CTA
Who in your organization is specifically accountable for agent behavior? Not the project owner.
Not the IT sponsor. Accountable for what the agent does on Tuesday at 3am.
