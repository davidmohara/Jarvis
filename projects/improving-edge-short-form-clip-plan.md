The gap: brand-voice clips underperform across the consultancy set, the leadership hosts prove the format works at 10-100x the traction, and no one puts a named operator with AI delivery credibility on camera. That seat is open.

David is the only voice in this set who runs a regional P&L, delivers AI work to enterprise clients daily, teaches executives AI in paid workshops, and brings a culture-informed executive voice without being preachy about it. The big firms can't put a named accountable operator on camera; the leadership clip machines can't talk about AI with any depth; the AI shows have no face. Short-form lets him own the intersection: the operator's 60 seconds on AI in real organizations.
