The AI conversation spends its time on models and misses what actually determines who wins: the economics. Pricing models are being redesigned under your feet, the platform war has already settled, and the physical layer, power, data centers, capital, is now the constraint that decides who ships. This page collects my running notes on AI economics, what it costs, how it is priced, where the money is flowing, and what the build-out means for the places watching it happen, including my own region.
