## Patterns Identified

1. **Daily-review scheduled phantom success is chronic and worsening.** 11 of 28 runs (39%) are F-grade phantom completions. Jul 10 clean run (interactive, 14:05 local) broke the streak; Jul 11 scheduled run (02:11 UTC) immediately reverted. The pattern is timing-consistent: scheduled 4 AM CDT runs produce phantoms, interactive runs produce real output. Root cause likely remains the stale session_id not refreshing between scheduled runs (identified Jul 8).

2. **system-eval-001 assertion is structurally flawed — 6 false failures.** The glob-based check matches the current in-progress run's record as the "most recent" file, which has status: in-progress, not success. This is a false negative on every active run. Affects 6 of 10 system-eval records.

3. **close-eval-record.py session_id mismatch creates spurious records on every system-eval run.** VFJBTD (Jul 8) is the second confirmed spurious record. The script creates a fresh record instead of closing the existing stub when session IDs don't match. The step-06 fallback (direct JSON write) is a workaround but leaves the incorrect stub open unless manually closed.

4. **Morning-briefing mechanical partial is a false negative — 4/5 runs affected.** Output quality grades are A:3, B:1, C:1. The partial status is a step-registration instrumentation issue, not a real delivery failure. Fixing this would raise morning-briefing's average score from 0.747 to approximately 0.91.

5. **Controller feedback coverage remains structurally thin at 13%.** 47 records have null ratings. All 7 rated records are positive — no negative feedback in the full corpus. Tier 4 contributes nothing to 87% of composite scores.

6. **Jul 9 and Jul 11 phantom failures bracket the Jul 10 clean run.** Jul 10 ran at 14:05 local (likely interactive). Jul 9 and Jul 11 ran at 02:1x UTC (scheduled). Three consecutive records confirm the interactive vs. scheduled split is the discriminating factor.

7. **General-purpose has no real runs — only aborted stubs dragging corpus averages.** All 3 records (avg score 0.327) are session interruptions with no steps executed. They add noise to corpus-level stats without representing real workflow performance.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the daily-review scheduled runner session context.** Inspect how Chief's scheduled trigger constructs its session_id and ensure it generates a fresh value on each invocation. The interactive runs work; the scheduled runs don't. This is the highest-priority fix in the system — 39% phantom failure rate on a daily workflow.

2. **Redesign system-eval-001 to exclude in-progress records.** Change the check to filter for status:success in the glob results (e.g., check the second-most-recent file, or filter by status field). Alternatively, change it to check the prior run's state.yaml for status:complete rather than grepping the runs directory. 6 false failures per 10 runs is structural noise.

3. **Fix close-eval-record.py to match by eval_id, not session_id.** Pass the eval_id explicitly to the script so it closes the correct stub regardless of session context. This eliminates the spurious record creation pattern that has now produced 2 phantom records (VUBDSE's VFJBTD, and this run's anticipated duplicate).
