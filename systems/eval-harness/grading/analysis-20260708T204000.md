## Patterns Identified

1. **Daily-review phantom success — worsening trend.** 9 of 25 runs (36%) have duration_seconds: 0.0, status: success, and 0/8 assertions passing. The July 7 and July 8 runs are the most recent. Both KHEVMG and 5P65RK share session_id session-2026-07-06-095100 — a session created on June 24 that is clearly stale. The scheduled runner is firing the instrumentation wrapper but not actually executing the workflow body. This has been flagged in every prior analysis report since 2026-07-04 with no fix deployed.

2. **System-eval is the most reliable workflow.** 7 of 8 runs score 0.88+. The one outlier (PCHVJ1, score 0.39) was an early aborted run from May 27. The July 4 run (1D1QL1) scored a perfect 1.0. System-eval is functioning as designed.

3. **Morning-briefing has a persistent mechanical partial.** All 5 morning-briefing runs are partial — mechanical.completed is false on every run. Despite this, structural assertions pass and grades are B or A. The partial status is either a mis-classification in the instrumentation or an expected characteristic of the workflow that hasn't been corrected. Early signal only (5 runs).

4. **Tier 4 feedback gap is total.** Zero feedback across 49 records. The feedback weight is effectively dead weight in the scoring model for this corpus. The workflow with the most to gain from signal is daily-review (25 runs, high variance, many F grades with no human confirmation).

5. **General-purpose aborted stubs are unresolvable.** 3 records (2Y8GSF, BQEM4B, WVFASP) are aborted ad-hoc sessions with no assertion definitions, no output files, and no steps tracked. They have no path to a passing grade and drag the corpus average down. These are candidates for archival.

6. **Daily-review bimodal performance.** When daily-review actually runs, it scores well (avg 0.88 for runs with assertion_rate > 0). When the phantom-success pattern fires, it scores 0.33-0.47. The workflow itself is competent — the delivery failure is entirely in the scheduling/session layer.

7. **No assertion definitions for 5+ workflows.** genius-spark-meeting-prep, general-purpose, remarkable-upload, content-approval, partner-meeting-prep, and plaud-ingest have either no definition or minimal assertion coverage. As one-off or low-frequency workflows, this is acceptable — but any that run more than twice should have definitions added.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the daily-review scheduled runner immediately.** The stale session_id (session-2026-07-06-095100) shared across the July 7 and July 8 runs is the smoking gun — the scheduler is not creating a fresh session context for each run. Check how Chief's daily-review workflow is invoked in the scheduler and ensure session_id is generated at invocation time, not inherited from a prior session. This is the highest-priority fix in the system.

2. **Add a zero-duration guard to daily-review close-eval-record instrumentation.** If `completed == started` and `duration_seconds == 0.0`, override `status` to `failure` instead of `success`. This would correctly classify 9 runs that are currently misreported as successes and polluting the corpus.

3. **Collect Tier 4 feedback on at least 5 daily-review runs.** Start with: XNRG5T (score 0.51, F), CFZ2ZA (score 0.33, F), VB6Z9C (score 0.76, F), MKO2P3 (score 0.47, F), and ZQTYH1 (score 1.0, A — positive baseline). Even a handful of ratings would activate the feedback component of the scoring model.

4. **Investigate morning-briefing partial status.** Read the morning-briefing workflow file and determine whether `mechanical.completed: false` is expected behavior or a bug. If it's always partial by design, update the assertion definition to reflect this so the tier 1 weight doesn't penalize every run.

5. **Archive the 3 general-purpose aborted stubs** (2Y8GSF, BQEM4B, WVFASP) to `runs/archive/`. They have no assertion definitions, no output, and no recovery path. Their presence in the corpus consistently pulls the batch average down and adds noise to the analysis without providing signal.

6. **Add assertion definitions for remarkable-upload and genius-spark-meeting-prep** if these workflows run again. Both have active records with failing grades (D and F respectively) and no structural verification in place.

7. **Add a system-eval assertion that checks for stale daily-review runs.** A check like `file_exists_pattern` on `runs/eval-*.json` for any record where `name == daily-review` and `duration_seconds == 0.0` within the last 7 days would surface this failure mode automatically during every system-eval run.
