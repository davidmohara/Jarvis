42
correlation

The Department of Energy signed a landmark 123 nuclear cooperation agreement with Saudi Arabia, allowing the kingdom to explore uranium enrichment with US technology and expertise. The deal will last 30 years and now goes to Congress for a mandatory review period. Crucially, the agreement does not require Saudi Arabia to sign the IAEA's Additional Protocol — the 'gold standard' of international oversight that bipartisan US policy has traditionally demanded. Saudi Arabia could begin enrichment only after a joint US-Saudi feasibility study, but the deal gives Riyadh a path to domestic enrichment capability.

Both sides report the deal exists; left raises proliferation and policy-reversal alarms that right coverage largely omits
LEFT

Left outlets treat this as a significant and risky departure from US non-proliferation doctrine. CNN ran two pieces — one straight news, one framing the deal as 'a perfect metaphor for Trump's presidency' in its willingness to break precedent for short-term diplomatic wins.
