55
correlation

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported the US economy unexpectedly shed 23,000 jobs in July — economists had forecast a gain of 83,000 — while July CPI cooled slightly to 3.4% from 3.5% in June, aided by a 2.9% drop in gas prices. The unemployment rate dipped to 4.1%, but only because workers left the labor force. Wage growth sits at 3.2% annually, below the pace of inflation, meaning workers are losing purchasing power. The combination of job losses and sticky inflation has created a difficult policy moment for the Federal Reserve ahead of its September meeting.
