## Marketing/Personas (persona and anti-persona subfolders)

Used by `offering-match` to attach a buyer persona (Service line match) and an anti-buyer persona to each matched offering, with their "How Improving Wins" / "How Improving Disarms" responses. Two subfolders, `Buyer Personas` and `Anti-Buyer Personas`, under `OfficeoftheChiefConsultingOfficer/Shared Documents/General/Marketing/Personas/`.
