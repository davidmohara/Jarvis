### AI Meets the Law: In-House Counsel on Risk, Policy, and the New Legal Economics

## Episode Overview

We'll dig into the legal side of AI adoption with someone living it daily: what companies actually risk when employees use AI tools, what a real AI usage policy has to cover, and how to govern shadow AI without killing the energy. We'll also get the buyer's view on the shift from hourly to outcome-based legal billing, and why AI elevates paralegals instead of replacing them.
