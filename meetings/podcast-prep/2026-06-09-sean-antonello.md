## Key Topics and Talking Points

### 1. AI Risk Isn't Where Leaders Think It Is
- Most executives think AI risk = security/privacy. The real risk is financial bleed and false ROI signals.
- "AI theater": spending that looks like progress but produces no durable outcomes
- Where companies unintentionally burn money on AI initiatives
- How to tell real ROI from vanity metrics

### 2. Operational Readiness: What AI Exposes
- AI doesn't create weak processes: it amplifies and accelerates them
- Common signs an organization's ops aren't ready for AI
- The new operational risks AI introduces (data dependencies, hallucination in workflows, speed vs. stability)
- Balancing adoption speed vs. operational stability

### 3. Competitive Risk: Moving Too Slow vs. Too Fast
- The recklessness spectrum: what does going too fast actually look like?
- Companies overestimating AI's competitive moat
- The real competitive risk: falling behind on the *right* things while chasing the wrong ones

### 4. Governance Gaps
- Shadow AI: employees adopting AI faster than leadership can guide
- Most common governance gaps right now
- How small governance failures compound into enterprise-level problems
- What "healthy AI risk posture" actually looks like

### 5. Leadership Blind Spots
- Why AI surfaces leadership blind spots faster than most other tools
- The "we're probably fine" problem, and what to check first
- Lowest-effort, highest-impact moves to reduce AI risk quickly
- Where to focus in the next 90 days

### 6. Where SAP Gets It Right
- What SAP is actually delivering well in the AI/analytics space
- Capabilities executives may be underrating or overlooking
- How SAP's approach differs from the broader AI hype cycle
- What a realistic, high-value SAP AI use case looks like today

### 7. Reframing AI Evaluation
- The mental model most executives use when evaluating AI (and why it's wrong)
- What the right frame looks like: outcomes over features, data readiness over vendor promises
- How to ask better questions when a vendor pitches AI capabilities
- The shift from "what can AI do" to "what is my organization ready for"

## Prompting Questions (Distilled from Janine's Question Doc)

**Source:** Season 1_Episode 9_Topics and Questions.docx (SharePoint: Podcast Planning)

These are distilled from 19 questions down to 7 discussion prompts + wrap-up:

1. **On financial risk:** "Everyone talks about AI as a growth driver, but from your experience, where does risk actually show up first? And how is it different from traditional tech risk?"

2. **On operations:** "You've worked on both sides: finance and architecture. When you look at an organization's operations through an AI lens, what does 'not ready' actually look like?"

3. **On governance:** "Shadow AI is a real thing right now: employees just going around leadership to use AI tools. Is this getting better or worse, and what separates companies that handle it well from those that don't?"

4. **On competitive risk:** "There's this pressure to move fast. What does recklessness actually look like with AI adoption, and are companies more at risk of moving too fast or too slow right now?"

5. **On leadership:** "AI seems to expose leadership blind spots really quickly. Why is that, and if an executive finds themselves thinking 'we're probably fine,' what should they check first?"

6. **On where SAP gets it right:** "You live in the SAP analytics world. Setting aside the hype, where is SAP actually getting AI right? What should executives be paying closer attention to that they might be underrating?"

7. **On reframing AI evaluation:** "If you could hand executives one mental model shift for how they evaluate AI in their analytics stack, what would it be? What are most of them getting wrong in how they even frame the question?"

8. **Wrap-Up:** "What are a couple of takeaways you'd like the listeners to walk away with?"
