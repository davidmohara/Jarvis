### The New Leadership Skill: Working with AI

## Episode Overview

Most of the conversation about AI centers on keeping a "human in the loop." Claudio flips that framing: it's really AI in the human's loop. We'll explore what that means for how teams actually work, why people who coach and bring clarity are adapting to AI faster, and how leading AI agents gives everyone a path to practice leadership. We'll close on the human side of AI — how the biggest multiplier isn't technical, it's the stories AI helps us find and tell.
