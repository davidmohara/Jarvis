### The CFO's Ledger: AI Spend, Org ROI, and What Comes After the Mania

## Episode Overview

We'll talk with Improving's own CFO about the spend-versus-value math of AI from inside a company that sells AI delivery: thirty years of big-company discipline, a SPAC listing at the peak of the mania, and what PE speed taught him about what happens when the math arrives. We'll also get his first public take on the Texas Stock Exchange and what the North Texas build-out means for the CFO function.

## Prompting Questions

**1. On Discipline and the Mania**

> *"Thirty years at P&G, Kellogg, Diageo, then a SPAC listing in 2021 at the peak of the window, then PE-backed Improving. What did that window teach you about how markets price a story versus how they price the math?"*

> *→ What did you have to unlearn the week you arrived at a PE-backed company?*

**2. On AI in His Own House**

> *"You've called AI a very hungry monster: feed it data, it wants more. You run a finance team of about 40. Where has AI actually earned its keep, and what did you refuse to fund?"*

> *→ A board member says refusals are how you fall behind. What do you tell them?*

**3. On the Org ROI Math**

> *"Not sales ROI, the organization's ROI: payback horizon, avoided cost versus new capability, productivity promise versus headcount reality. How do you run that math when a president walks in with an AI investment?"*

> *→ You watched a mania get repriced in real time. Is AI spend on the same curve?*

**4. On Y'all Street**

> *"The TXSE is live, Schwab and Texas Capital are primary-listed, Cullen/Frost is leaving the NYSE. You've never spoken publicly about it. As a CFO who has taken a company through a listing decision, what convinces you a new exchange is durable?"*

**5. On What Comes Next**

> *"Morgan Stanley's tower, NYSE's Texas HQ, DFW now the number two financial jobs market. What does North Texas look like in five years, and what does the CFO function look like in three: agentic close, audit prep, all of it?"*

**6. Wrap-Up**
