### Nearshore/Offshore and AI: Rethinking How and Where Work Gets Done

## Episode Overview

AI is reshaping distributed delivery models in ways most organizations haven't fully reckoned with. David Sandoval leads Improving Mexico and has spent 14 years building nearshore teams. We'll explore whether AI is reducing the need for distributed teams or making them more important, how collaboration and knowledge-sharing across regions is changing, and what separates teams truly leveraging AI from those just experimenting with it.
