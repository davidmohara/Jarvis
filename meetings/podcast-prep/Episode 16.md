<h4>INTRO SCRIPT</h4>

Welcome to **The Improving Edge**. This is where we talk about how AI and technology actually show up in real organizations; what works, what doesn't, and what we're learning along the way. I'll be sharing conversations with people doing the work, turning innovation into outcomes.

### Banking on AI: A Chief Banking Officer's View from Inside the Platform Build

## Episode Overview

We'll dig into what actually changes inside a regional bank once a real AI platform is in place, not on a roadmap. We'll cover how First United sequenced infrastructure against real banking problems, why they invested in AI guardrails early, and what it takes to sustain a multi-phase AI partnership instead of restarting with a new vendor every round.

## Prompting Questions

**1. On Platform vs. Roadmap**

> *"You've been working on building out FUB's AI platform, what has actually changed in the bank's operations as that has gotten in place, versus when it was still a project on a roadmap?"*

> *"Have you found certain use cases that are easier to build than others, or that have higher ROI than others?"*

**2. On Sequencing**

> *"Did y'all just decide to "go AI", or did specific banking problems pull that decision into existence?"*

**3. On Guardrails First**

> *"You invested in an AI Data Loss Prevention layer alongside the platform build. That's not a typical first move. What was the risk conversation that made DLP a priority rather than an afterthought?"*

**4. On Regulator Confidence**

> *"Regulators are still catching up to how banks use AI in decisioning and customer-facing workflows, how has that structure changed how you engage examiners?"*

**5. On Accountability**

> *"You're embedding outside engineers directly on your AI platform team rather than keeping the work fully internal. How do you think about accountability when the people building it aren't all FUB employees?"*

**6. On the Customer Payoff**

> *"Your public-facing modernization, the unified portal, digital mortgage, sits on top of this AI and data infrastructure. Walk us through the connection: what does the deep learning program actually make possible there that wasn't possible before?"*

**7. On Relationship Banking**

> *"FUB has always been a people-first organization - the values, culture, and community commitment are obviously there. If AI takes over more of the back-office and decisioning work, what happens to the relationship-first model that First United has built its reputation on?"*

**8. On Team and Skills**

> *"How has this AI platform work changed what you're hiring for, or what skills you're developing in your existing team?"*

> *"What's the learning curve been like for your people as they've had to operate this infrastructure day-to-day?"*

**9. On Advice**

> *"If you had one piece of advice for another bank leader about to start this kind of platform investment, infrastructure first, guardrails early, partner selection, what's the one thing you'd tell them not to skip?"*

**10. Wrap-Up**

> *"What are a couple of takeaways you'd like the listeners to walk away with?"*
