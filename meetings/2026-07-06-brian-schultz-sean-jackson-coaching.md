## EasyBotChat — What It Is

AI-powered staff assistant for multi-location operations. Employees get instant answers to operational questions via mobile (or NFC tags placed at key locations in the building). Trained on company SOPs, policies, and training materials.

**Key differentiators:**
- Flat monthly fee, no per-user or per-location charges (scales cleanly)
- Turnkey setup, live in one week
- Analytics dashboard showing what teams are actually asking (surfaces training gaps)
- Built for hospitality and retail, now expanding into broader markets

**LOOK Cinemas results (their own case study):** Cut training time, reduced manager repetitive inquiries, improved consistency across all locations, reduced turnover signal.

**Just announced:** Strategic partnership with Cinema Hero Solutions (CinemaCon 2026) — named as preferred deployment partner for cinema operators. Cinema Hero has 50+ years combined experience in POS deployments and theater operations consulting. This is EasyBot's first major industry distribution channel.

## The Coaching Context

Brian is running two companies simultaneously. The coaching relationship is the core of this meeting — David provides leadership counsel and strategic sounding board. A few themes that are almost certainly in play:

**Dual-hat leadership**
Brian is CEO of LOOK (an operating company with ~1,000 employees) while also building EasyBot as a startup from the ground up. These are two very different leadership modes. The CinemaCon moment gave EasyBot public credibility and the Cinema Hero partnership gives them distribution. The pressure to decide how much of himself to put into each company is real.

**EasyBot growth stage**
They've got a proven case study (LOOK), a major partnership (Cinema Hero), and fresh press coverage. The next question is go-to-market at scale: Do they stay in cinema or use it as the beachhead for broader hospitality/retail? Who sells? How does Sean's technical capacity hold up as deployments grow?

**Team and talent**
Both companies have people challenges. LOOK runs ~1,000 employees in high-turnover hospitality. EasyBot is a small startup likely needing to hire as they scale. This is a recurring leadership challenge Brian lives every day.
