## 2. Company Overview

**Southern Methodist University (SMU)** is a private research university in Dallas, Texas. The **Cox School of Business** is its business school and consistently ranks among the top business schools in the Southwest. Cox houses the ITOM Department, which is the academic home for the new AI for Business program.

**MS in AI for Business (MSAIB)** is a new STEM-designated graduate degree announced by SMU Cox on August 5, 2025, with its inaugural cohort starting Fall 2026. Key program facts:

- **Format:** 9-month full-time or 21-month part-time
- **Credits:** 32 credit hours
- **Curriculum focus:** Predictive AI, generative AI, machine learning, LLM technology, AI ethics, business implementation, and data analytics
- **Tools covered:** Python, R, SAS, Tableau, Alteryx, Hadoop, Spark, NodeXL, Google Analytics
- **Design intent:** Bridge technical AI competency with business strategy and implementation leadership
- **Career outcomes:** Graduates targeting roles in analytics, AI consulting, data-driven leadership; prior cohorts from related programs have landed at Accenture, AT&T, Capital One, Amazon, EY, and PwC
- **Undergraduate pathway:** A 4+1 degree option exists for SMU undergraduates

The **ITOM Advisory Board** is an established industry advisory body for the department. Known current or recent members include executives from Oncor Electric, Mazda/Toyota, Lennox International, and Southwest Airlines. The MSAIB program may have its own emerging advisory board separate from or nested within the ITOM board. Vishal Ahuja (Advisory Board Member, TraumaCare.AI) has been referenced in connection with the program. The full MSAIB advisory board roster is not publicly listed.

The program is designed to leverage Dallas's status as a growing technology and business hub, and SMU Cox is explicitly positioning it as a regional talent pipeline for AI-capable business leaders.
