## Recommended Outline (90 minutes)

### 1. Open and anchor the arc (10 min)
Stats and stories on AI and workforce: reduction, training, adoption. Set up the core tension the session will resolve.

### 2. Frame the problem (15 min)
What Improving is seeing in the market: adoption is outpacing readiness. This is why Improving's offerings address the mental/change side as much as the technical side — readiness is the bottleneck, not access to tools.

### 3. Frameworks (20 min)
Introduce Improving's frameworks for thinking about where and how AI can be applied: the maturity model, and task compression / demand limits. This is the credibility anchor and the mental model participants carry into the exercise.

### 4. Toolkit handoff — use case mapping exercise (20 min)
The call to action. Each participant maps one real use case (onboarding, candidate search, job descriptions, etc.) against:
- What are the discrete steps?
- What systems do they touch?
- How often do you do it?
- How long does it take right now?

Then apply the framework from step 3 to identify where AI can be inserted into that workflow.

### 5. Table talk bridge (15 min)
Participants share their mapped use case with the table. Surfaces patterns across companies/industries and shows people what others are aiming at.

### 6. Close and connect to capstone (10 min)
Instruct participants to remeasure their use case after 30 days: did the AI-applied step get faster/better? Multiply the per-instance gain by frequency to quantify the real impact. Tie this measurement habit back to the cohort's company-specific talent action plans.
