## Company Overview

The **Dallas Regional Chamber** is the principal economic development engine for the 13-county DFW region — 700+ member companies, four strategic pillars, funded through the **Tomorrow Fund** investor community.

**Four pillars:**
1. Prosperity & Economic Development — HQ recruiting, business retention, expansion
2. Education, Talent & Workforce — pipeline development, Say Yes to Dallas campaign
3. Public Policy — advocacy at city, state, and federal levels
4. Quality of Life — livability, equity, community investment

**Current posture in 2026:**
- DFW led the nation in corporate relocations — 100+ HQ moves 2018–2024, pace accelerating
- AI and tech are the defining story: 19,000 AI professionals in DFW (larger than Austin), $1.1B+ in startup investment (2024), Google's $40B Texas data center commitment is live
- The DRC is actively positioning DFW as the premier AI infrastructure destination — which is both a recruiting story and an investor story

**The physical infrastructure is landing, not just the pitch.** The compute layer is going up: Wistron (Nvidia supplier) chose Fort Worth over every other city in the country for its first U.S. AI supercomputing facilities — $761M, 1.1 million sq ft, 800 jobs at AllianceTexas, 20 minutes from where most regional clients operate. Lambda secured $700M for a facility in Plano. Texas has 248 data centers in the pipeline statewide. AI engineering is now the fastest-growing job category in DFW. The "emerging market" thesis is done — it arrived.

**One headwind worth knowing:** Governor Abbott recently ordered utilities to charge data centers for their own grid infrastructure costs, and AI regulation is now a stated 2027 legislative priority. Texas's frictionless-expansion advantage is under pressure. This is early innings, but the era of zero-friction AI buildout in Texas may be shorter than the headline numbers suggest — which creates a consulting angle (power strategy, regulatory risk) that's just starting to surface in client conversations.

**Tomorrow Fund** is the DRC's primary funding vehicle — companies invest in it to support the DRC's work. Improving may already be in this ecosystem (see sponsorship thread). Understanding what Mike is trying to build through the Fund is worth exploring.
