- Lawyers have been disbarred for citing cases that don't exist (AI hallucinations)
- Judge names, docket numbers, legal codes must be 100% correct
- Not just about "using Claude for quick case searches" — right tool for right situation
- Thompson Reuters/Co Counsel differentiator: correctness-first approach

- Discussion of what's worked well, where course corrections were needed
- Part of broader organizational AI adoption challenge

### 4. Talent Transformation (Hot Topic)
- Creating new job profiles in an AI world
- How hiring, training, coaching, performance management must change
- Career development and progression models shifting
- Tosan sees this as the most critical blocker for AI adoption at scale

### 5. Customer Expectations & Delivery
- Legal tech customers expect vendors to "meet needs" with AI (not just talk about it)
- Thompson Reuters positioning on timing and market announcements
- Competitive field requires speed without sacrificing quality
